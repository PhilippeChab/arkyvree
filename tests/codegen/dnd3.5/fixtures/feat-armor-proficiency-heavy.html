Armor Proficiency (heavy)

( Player's Handbook v.3.5, p. 89)

[General]

You are proficient with heavy armor (see Table 7-6: Armor and Shields, page 123).

Prerequisite

Armor Proficiency (light), Armor Proficiency (medium),

Required for

Axespike (RS) , Heavy Armor Optimization (RS) , Greater Heavy Armor Optimization (RS) ,

Benefit

See Armor Proficiency (light).

Special

Fighters, paladins, and clerics automatically have Armor Proficiency (heavy) as a bonus feat. They need not select it.

Normal

See Armor Proficiency (light).

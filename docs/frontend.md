# Frontend

How the client (`client/src/`) is written. Read it before writing or changing client code: every pattern the client follows is here, with the component or hook that is its one way and the lint rule that holds it.

Lint holds what it can, with no exception (`bun run lint`: the `arkyvree/*` rules of `lint/frontend.mjs`, `lint/components.mjs`, `lint/controls.mjs`, `lint/styles.mjs`, `lint/wording.mjs` and `lint/feedback.mjs`, the import rules of `.oxlintrc.json`, and the repo's general ones in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)). What lint can't judge is decided here: which empty state a place takes, a framed or a bare table, which dialog wrapper. A look the app doesn't have yet is the owner's call: propose it, never invent one, and never flatten one place's look into another's unasked.

## Before finishing

- TypeScript passes: `bunx tsgo --noEmit -p .`
- `bun run lint` passes (`bun run lint --fix` writes what it can), and `bun run format:check` (`bunx oxfmt` the changed files, never a directory)
- The impacted tests pass: `bun run test:changed`, and `tests/client` for the client's pure modules
- A change users see is looked at: the screens it touches, at a phone's width (375px) and a desktop's, in both themes

## A page

- Named `XPage.tsx` under `pages/`, its default export `export default function XPage` (`arkyvree/component-files`): `XDetailsPage` for a record's own page (`RulesetDetailsPage`, `EntityDetailsPage`), and a page that shows a record another way is named for that way (`SharedCharacterPage`, `CampaignCharacterPage`, `CustomizationPage`). `App.tsx` imports it lazily, a chunk of its own, but the two pages a visit lands on, `SignInPage` and `DashboardPage`, which it imports eagerly
- It names the browser tab with `usePageTitle(title)` (a simple entity's through the shell it renders, `RulesetEntityDetail`), which the app's name follows (`APP_NAME`, `lib/brand.ts`)
- Its column is a `Container` of the app's page width, its default (`lg`, 1200px, where `AppMain` caps every page): the lists and the records, the dashboard, a shared sheet, the account and activity pages, an entity page (`EntityDetailLayout`'s) alike, and no `maxWidth` of its own but a narrower one's: `"md"` for the legal page, which reads as text, and `"sm"` for an invite's card (`InvitePage`); an auth page stands in `AuthPage`'s panel. A tab's content fills it (`SectionContent`). The theme pads a `Container` 32px at its top and its end (`MuiContainer`), on a phone too: a page starts 32px under the app bar, and its column's `sx` never sets its top, but an invite's, whose card stands alone, centred (`arkyvree/page-gaps`)
- It fades in through `PageTransition`, around its loaded content, every state an invite's page shows too: its loader and its error render outside it (`EntityDetailLayout` and the auth pages' `AuthPage` carry it for theirs), and nothing inside it fades in again
- It opens on its header, its content 32px below (`arkyvree/page-gaps`): `PageHeader` for a list, an account, an activity or the legal page (`variant="tinted"` on the content lists, the rulesets, characters and campaigns; its default `hero` on the others), `DetailPageHeader` for a ruleset or a campaign, `CharacterHeader` for a character's sheet, `EntityDetailLayout` for a ruleset entity's page (a simple entity's, a class's, a customization's), which renders `DetailPageHeader` (a Back with nowhere to go yet hides: no `backTo`). Three pages open on a header of their own: the dashboard on its hero banner (`DashboardPage`), the auth pages on `AuthPage`'s title (`components/auth/AuthPage.tsx`; on a phone the layout's panel frames it under the brand, and it draws none of its own), an invite on its card (`InvitePage`, `pages/invites`, which each invite's route renders with its `kind`, what it shows of each kind beside it, `INVITE_PAGES`: its title, its icon, what it's to, the invite it reads). Its title, its `h1`, is its header's alone (`arkyvree/page-titles`)
- It shows `PageLoader` while it first loads (a character sheet's page, the character's, a campaign's or a share link's, draws `CharacterDetailSkeleton` instead, `arkyvree/page-loaders`, and `EntityDetailLayout`'s `isLoading` a skeleton of itself), and `PageError` when it fails with nothing loaded to keep (`EntityPageError` on an entity page: a `PageError` in `EntityDetailLayout`'s column), never an alert of its own: its Back is named for where it goes, "Back to Campaigns" (`arkyvree/page-errors`). An entity page goes back to the list it was opened from, a ruleset's tab, a class's or another entity's customization tab: `entityPageBack(location.state, fallback, words)` gives both the place and its name (an entity's in its ruleset's words, just "Back" until they're in), its fallback what holds the entity (a class level's class, a modifier's entity) or its list, and its error goes back the same way (`arkyvree/entity-pages`). A part of a page that fails on its own shows a `LoadError` where it shows (a profile's linked accounts, the legal page's license, the dashboard's statistics). An archived record's page (a ruleset's, a campaign's, a character's) says so above its tabs or its sheet with `ArchivedNotice` (`what`, and `canUnarchive` for whom its menu offers Unarchive)

## Recipes

How the app's recurring screens are put together. Build a new one the same way, from the same pieces.

- **A list page** (the rulesets, characters, campaigns, notifications, activity): its search and sort come from `useListParams(sortFields, defaultSort)`, its other filters from the URL too (`updateSearchParams`, read back through `oneOf`), and its list from `useListPageQuery(xListQuery(filters))`: `lib/queries.ts`'s for the lists the sidebar warms (`rulesetListQuery`, `characterListQuery`, `campaignListQuery`), its page's own `…Queries.ts` for the others (`activityQueries.ts`, `notificationQueries.ts`). It opens on its list's defaults, beside its query (`RULESET_LIST_DEFAULTS`, `CHARACTER_LIST_DEFAULTS`, `CAMPAIGN_LIST_DEFAULTS`), the URL's when it says none, which the sidebar warms too. Under its `PageHeader` stand its `SearchBar` and its `ListPageResults`, a column of their own (`spacing={3}`): the first load, the failure while nothing has loaded, `NoMatchesState` for a search, its own `empty` state (a `BlankState`), else its grid (`ListCardGrid`) or its table (`TableFrame`), and a large Load More; a tab's list that isn't a ruleset table (a campaign's characters and players, a ruleset's feats grouped by family) shows the same way, `variant="section"`: a section's spinner, or its table's `skeleton`, and a medium Load More. A searched list's results show through it or a ruleset tab's `RulesetSectionTable` alone, which say a search found nothing (`NoMatchesState`): no other module draws one (`arkyvree/list-results`). A card is a `ListCard`, staggered in from the query's `offset` (`animationIndex`, `animationOffset`); a character's shows its race and classes through `CharacterBuildChips` (`components/characters`), and a list of characters its portraits through `useCharacterPortraits`
- **A record page with tabs** (`RulesetDetailsPage`, `CampaignDetailsPage`, `ProgressionOwnerPage`, `CustomizationPage`): its tab is the URL's `:section` segment (`rulesets/:id/:section` in `App.tsx`; a progression owner's, past its kind's segment and its id, `:tab`), and a URL without a known one renders a `<Navigate … replace />` to its first tab. `SectionTabs` warm a tab's data as it's hovered (`onTabHover`: the ruleset's `prefetchSection`, a progression owner's tab's `prefetch`, a customization tab's rows), and its content is a `SectionTabPanel`, 56px under them (`arkyvree/page-gaps`). Its actions are its header's menu: `DetailPageHeader`'s `onMenuOpen` is `menu.openMenu` (`useAnchorMenu()`) when the session has something to do there, else `undefined`, which hides the button; each item is an `ActionMenuItem` (an action's `onClick={menu.closeMenuAnd(() => …)}`, a page of the app's `to` or an outside page's `href` with `onClick={menu.closeMenu}`, `pending` while the request it started runs). The ruleset page keeps its dialogs, forms and mutations in an operations hook, `useRulesetOperations` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`); the other record pages hold theirs, and a list's create dialog holds its own form and mutation, mounted as it opens (`CreateCharacterDialog`, `CreateCampaignDialog`; a new character's lays out its rules' form, `characterForm`, its picked ruleset's base rules', its fields' values by their keys, `newCharacter.ts`). What the session may do comes from `useRulesetPermissions(ruleset)`, `useCampaignPermissions(campaign)` or `useCharacterPermissions(character)` (`pages/characters/details`): an action shows only when it's allowed, and a dialog's or a notice's rights are its too, never worked out where they're passed (`ContributorsDialog`'s `canInvite`, `canLeave` and `canRemove`, `ArchivedNotice`'s `canUnarchive`)
- **A ruleset section tab** (`pages/rulesets/details/sections/`), shown where its rules' `kinds` list it, in their order, named in their words, its picture by the generic name they give it (`kindIcon`), its URL segment its type: a component of `RulesetSectionProps` (`ruleset`, `childOnly`, `onChildOnlyChange`; `pages/rulesets/details/sectionProps.ts`) in a `SectionContent`, every ruleset's. A kind's tab is `EntitySection`, which its kind's description drives (`EntityKindDescription`: what its row opens, `opens`, its customization page, its own page or a class's progression; whether what a race or a class is for narrows it and it sorts, `partitioned`, through `useEntityFilters`; a form writes none, `readOnly`, the abilities', which have no search either, their Local Changes alone in a `ListToolbar`; its blank state's words, `empty`; its create form's room for a long description, `longDescription`, a mechanic's, each field's help, `fieldHelp`, a class's, and a wide dialog, `wideForm`, an item's, a spell's; the pool a picker narrows it by, `pools`, and a spell's level, `levels`, a spell's; its variants grouped under their family, `families`, a feat's, an `EntityGroupsTable` whose family rows expand into their variants, which Group Families toggles off; and its rows' Duplicate and Create Variants, `copies`, an item's, the duplicate's form keeping its kind's locked columns, the variants made in `EntityVariantsDialog` through `useEntityVariants`). Every tab but Settings is one. Its columns are its list's (`useEntityList(rulesetId, entityType)` over `RulesDescription.lists`: its name's share, its own columns between its name and its description, each read by `ListValue` where the engine says it's held (its own field, `field`, among the entity's `fields`; or its links of a `role`, among its `links`; which names its column in the table too, `listColumnId`) and as it says it reads, a chip of its label (a hit die's "d10"), its text as it is or quiet, a number or a decimal in its unit, the name of the entity it names, a flag's "Yes", a template, the pools it's picked in; and the entities its reference columns name, whose failure it shows), its search the URL's (`useEntityFilters`, whose kind and sort a partitioned kind's list takes too; none on a read-only kind), its list an infinite query of `sectionQueries.ts` through the routes by kind (`entitiesQuery(ruleset.id, kind, { search, childOnly })`) read with `pageItems`, and its create `useRulesetSection`'s (below), its create dialog `<CreateDialog {...createDialogProps} title="Create New Language">` around its form fields (`RulesetEntityFormFields`); an entity is edited and deleted on its own page. Its toolbar is its `SectionSearchBar` (`pages/rulesets/components`): its `SearchBar`, its own toggles and Local Changes on a fork as its `actions` (`SectionActions`, which a tab without a search puts in a `ListToolbar` alone: the abilities'), its add for an editor (`addLabel`, `onAdd`), and `filtered` while its filters narrow the list (an aptitude, a spell level); its table a `RulesetSectionTable` (`what`, `error`, `search`, `columns`, `renderCell`, and its `empty` state, a `BlankState`, as a list page's `ListPageResults` takes it; `onRowClick` opens the entity through `useOpenEntity`, `onRowPrefetch` warms its page (`useRowPrefetch`); `onEdit`, `onDuplicate` and `onDelete` with `canEdit` give a row its actions, where a table has them: the items' Duplicate, a customization tab's Edit and Delete); then a `LoadMoreButton`. The Settings tab (`SettingsSection`) lists nothing: it's the ruleset's own fields, its Settings, in a `Panel` holding their form (`EntityFields` of the `rulesets` type, each saying what it's for, `help`), read from `rulesetSettingsQuery` and saved through `useEntityForm` (its `formKey` the ruleset's), the same fields read-only (`ReadOnlyFields`), with no Save, for who can't edit the ruleset's content
- **A ruleset entity's form** (`pages/rulesets/components/forms/`, every ruleset's): one form for every kind, which its create dialog and its page share: `EntityFormData`, its request's body (`entityForm.ts`: its name, description, its `fields` by stored field and its `links` by role, its columns among them where they're stored, as their inputs hold them: an item's cost and weight as their fields' text), `RulesetEntityFormFields({ form, entityType, rulesetId })`, its name and description bound to the form (`nameLocked`: a generated feat's; `longDescription`: a mechanic's), then `EntityFormFields`, and its empty values, `toEntityForm(columns)` (`entityForm.ts`, which makes an entity's own too, `toEntityForm(columns, entity)`, each column read where its rules say the entity holds it, its own field or its link of a role, `entityFields.ts`, and what its save sends, `toEntityBody(columns, data)`: a decimal its number, an optional choice, reference or template null for none, a column its form hides null). Its columns are its rules' to describe, never the client's (`RulesDescription.columns`, `GET /api/rules`, read through `useEntityColumns(rulesetId, entityType)`: a class's hit die, a race's size and speed, a skill's primary ability, a save's ability, a language's type, a feat's pools, a spell's save and spell lists, an item's cost, weight, type, slot and template, a level's saves and granted feats), each at its place in the body (`placeOf`: `fields.<its field>` or `links.<its role>`), and `EntityFormFields` lays them out around the fields its ruleset declares: their plain values before (`EntityColumns`, `placement="before"`, as `EntityFields` lays a field out, bound at its own path, a decimal in its unit, "Cost (gp)"), their lists of links after (`placement="after"`: a spell's aptitudes each at its level, `LeveledLinksInput`; a level's base per save, `AmountsInput`; its granted feats, `GrantsInput`: each of the kind its field references), an item's template a select of its type's templates (`TemplateInput`, which a change of its type empties, a new item dropping the old template's values, `useTemplateReset`), each reading what it needs of the entity (`ColumnContext`: whether it's new, the aptitudes and feats it holds, the columns it keeps, its saved template's name). A level's amounts go out whole (3.5's saves), every entity of the kind its `amounts` field references at its value, 0 unset (`useAllAmounts`, off those its input loaded). Beside its own columns, an entity's form shows the fields its ruleset declares for its type, as the API describes them, and holds them under `fields`, as its body carries them, each bound by its stored field (`fields.SPELL_SCHOOL`, `fields.WEAPON_BASE_DAMAGE`): `EntityFields({ form, entityType, rulesetId })` (`forms/EntityFields.tsx`) lays them out from `useEntityFields(rulesetId, entityType)` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`, over `rulesetFieldsQuery`, `details/rulesetQueries.ts`, every entity type's at once, `staleTime: FOREVER`), each by its input with one generic component per kind (a select of a closed list, with the value the saved form holds when its ruleset no longer lists it, its none "None", or "Default" beside a "None" value; several values as chips, a list's or free ones; a number in its bounds, `wholeNumberRules`; a progression under its label, its frequency (`Every N Levels`, 0 for none), then the levels it gives more at, each its level and its amount (none for one that gives one at most a level), added (`AddButton`, the first free level) and removed (`RowAction`), a level listed twice or giving past its bound saying why under it (`wholeNumberRules`' `checks`; the Settings' feats and ability increases); a yes or no, `SwitchField`, or a select of Yes, No and None for a three-state one; a text with its example; an entity of the ruleset, a select of `rulesetOptionsQuery`'s list), one a row, or two side by side past a phone's width in a form that lays its own out so (`columns={2}`: a spell's, an item's; a list of values, a progression and a group then the whole row), its yes-or-no switches first, together (side by side past a phone's width, one a line on it), before the fields one may reveal, a group under its `SubsectionTitle` with the fields its kind shows in it (an armor's max Dex bonus, held by the item itself: every value goes at its stored field, `fields.ARMOR_MAX_DEX`), each saying what it's for under it where its form's did (`help`: a class's, the Settings'), a field on conditions while the form's values at their paths meet each (`shownWhen`: among its values, or, `except`, not). No form names a field of its own: a ruleset adds, changes or removes one by its declarations. Its values come through `withEntityFields(values, fields, entity?)` (`forms/entityFields.ts`: a new entity's empty, `emptyFieldValues`, an entity's own off its description, `toFieldValues`; none until the fields load), and an edit sends the fields and links it changed alone, its columns whole (`useEntitySave`, by `changedValues` against the form's `baseline()`), so a value its ruleset no longer lists stays until it's changed. What customizes an entity (a modifier, a requirement) has its pieces in `components/customization`, beside its inputs (`ModifierFormFields`, `EMPTY_MODIFIER`)
- **An entity's page** (`KindEntityPage`, `pages/rulesets/details/`, at `rulesets/:id/:section/:entityId` and its `:tab`): the page its kind opens, by its kind's URL segment among every base rules' kinds: `ProgressionOwnerPage` (`pages/rulesets/details/owners/`, every name in it an owner's: `ownerId`, `ownerType`, `OwnerTab`) for a kind whose row opens its progression (`opens: "progression"`: 3.5's classes), its tab the URL's `:tab`, an address of a tab it had before opening the one its rules' progression names (`tabAliases`), its queries through its kind's routes (`ownerParam`, its kind's type), its texts in its kind's words; else `EntityDetailsPage`, which has no tab: its address with one goes to its page. **A simple entity's page** (`EntityDetailsPage`, `pages/rulesets/details/entities/`): the page of an entity of any kind whose row opens its own (`opens: "page"`: abilities, aptitudes, languages, mechanics, saves, skills), its kind by its URL segment once its ruleset is known (its rules are, the app having loaded them before any page), the entity read meanwhile (a segment no such kind has goes back to the ruleset). Another base rules adds nothing to the client.
- **Prefetching**: what a user is about to open is loaded as they point at it, through the factory its page renders with: a card on hover and focus (`ListCard`'s `onMouseEnter` and `onFocus`), a table's row through `useRowPrefetch` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`: 150ms into a hover, at once on focus), which `RulesetSectionTable`'s `onRowPrefetch` and the feats' grouped rows take, every row that opens a page giving one (`arkyvree/row-prefetch`), a fork's chip its parent's page (`RulesetFactChips` warms it itself), a tab through `SectionTabs`' `onTabHover`, and the sidebar's links on hover (`PREFETCHERS`, `components/layout/Layout.tsx`: a list's first page, under its `…_LIST_DEFAULTS`)
- **A mutation and the cache**: the query client (`lib/queryClient.ts`) never retries, keeps data fresh for `FIVE_MINUTES`, clears the cache when the signed-in user changes, and signs the session out on a 401 (an auth request's aside). A record's keys nest under its own (`QUERY_KEYS.rulesets.section(id, section)` is `["rulesets", "detail", id, section]`), so invalidating a key refreshes what's under it. A mutation's `onSuccess` says it in a toast, invalidates through its domain's helper, and closes its dialog: `invalidateRulesetEdit(queryClient, rulesetId, keys)` (`pages/rulesets/details/sectionQueries.ts`: the lists and the ruleset's Local Changes), `invalidateCharacter` (a character's sheet is invalidated through it alone, which refreshes its level-up too: `arkyvree/query-keys`) and `invalidateCharacterListings` (`lib/queries.ts`), `invalidateCampaignPlayers` (`pages/campaigns/details/sectionQueries.ts`); its `onError` is `snackbar.error(error, "Failed to …")`. A delete navigates away, then removes its record's queries (`queryClient.removeQueries`), so Back never renders it from the cache (`EntityDetailLayout`'s `deletion`, `CampaignDetailsPage`, `CharacterDetailsPage`), and so does leaving a record, which replaces its page in the history (`useContributors`' leave, by its kind's `detailKey`; a campaign's, `PlayersSection`), so Back never shows it with the rights its user had. Data that never goes stale on its own takes `staleTime: FOREVER` (`lib/durations.ts`)
- **Staggered entry**: a list's items fade up one after another, `fadeInUpSx(index, offset)` (`theme/animations.ts`) in an item's `sx` (a page's few blocks at its slow pace, `"slow"`: the auth pages' branding) (`ListCard`'s `animationIndex` and `animationOffset`, `RulesetSectionTable`'s rows and its `animationOffset`). A list that loads its pages animates only the page that came in: from `useListPageQuery`'s `offset` (`useStaggerAnimation`), or `itemsBeforeLastPage(data)` (`lib/pageItems.ts`) for another infinite query, a paged ruleset table's `animationOffset` (`arkyvree/list-results`)
- **Server events**: the server's events refresh the queries they change: `WebSocketProvider` (`contexts/`) invalidates the keys `EVENT_INVALIDATION_MAP` lists for an event (`notifications:updated`, `activities:updated`), where a new event's keys go, and every event's once the socket reconnects, catching up on what it missed: no query polls for them (the notification bell's neither)

## Mobile First

- Develop mobile first — all new components and layouts must work on small screens (375px) before scaling up
- Use `useIsMobile()` hook (`client/src/hooks/useIsMobile.ts`) for mobile-specific branching: true below MUI's `md` breakpoint (900px), a tablet held upright included
- Use MUI responsive sx props (`{ xs: ..., sm: ..., md: ... }`) over static values
- A table is a `DataTable` of `DataRow`s and `DataCell`s (`components/common`): from `sm` up, the table it is; on a phone, a list, as the design systems show a table there (Shopify Polaris' `list`), so no column scrolls out of view. Each `DataCell` says where it goes on a phone, its `slot` (`PhoneSlot`): the `primary` cell leads the item, a `trailing` one sits at the end of that line (the number a player reads: an attack's bonus, a skill's total; or the row's `RowActions`), `inline` cells (chips, a short text) and `labeled` ones (led by their column's name, its `label`: "Rank 6") run on the line under it, parted by a gap, each wrapping whole, a `block` one (a description, what a row expands to) takes a line of its own, and a `hidden` one stays out (an empty value, a description an item's page holds); a second line's cell or a `block` that holds a dash alone (`EmptyValue`) stays out too. A cell's or a row's own `sx` gives way on a phone to its slot's styles, but what it sets at a breakpoint (`fontWeight: { xs: 600 }`: a group's name). A cell with nothing to show on a phone is `hidden`, its slot read from the row (`slot={row.range ? "inline" : "hidden"}`). A ruleset's tabs read their chips as plain text on a phone, but a count at a line's end (`plainChips`: a size, a hit die, an aptitude, a level's feats name a value), where a role's or a status's chip keeps its color. A row naming an account that has both a username and an email address (a player, a contributor) names it on a phone by its email alone (`USERNAME_BESIDE_EMAIL_SX` over `EMAIL_AS_NAME_SX`). A cell that says itself shorter on a phone gives its `phoneChildren` ("2 lbs" for "2.00 lbs", `formatTrimmed`), and a table whose numbers read as one row (the sheet's Spells per Day) shows them on a phone as the sheet's armor classes do, in `ScoreBox`es, its `DataTable` from `sm` up alone, as its load under the equipment. MUI's `Table`, `TableBody`, `TableCell`, `TableContainer`, `TableHead` and `TableRow` are `DataTable`'s alone (`no-restricted-imports`), so every table lists on a phone
- A list's `SearchBar` is one row on a phone (`useIsMobile`): its search, a button that opens what narrows or orders the list (its `filters`, its toggles, its `actions`, and its filter and sort menus as `ChoiceChip`s) in a sheet risen from the screen's foot, named for what it holds ("Filters", "Sort", "Filter and Sort") and dotted while something narrows the list (`filtered`, or a filter menu off its first option), then its add, the `AddButton`'s icon alone (`compact`); from `md` up, its row as before, its add after its toggles
- The app paints under the system's edges (`viewport-fit=cover`, `client/index.html`): Android's gesture bar, an iPhone's home indicator and, held sideways, its notch. What sits at one of them keeps clear of it by its inset (`theme/safeArea.ts`), its background still painting under it: the page's area (`AppMain`), the sidebar, the app bar and a full-screen dialog (the theme's), a sheet risen from the screen's foot, the toasts. A new element fixed to the screen's foot or side does the same: `pb: (theme) => pastInset(theme.spacing(3), SAFE_BOTTOM)`, or `SAFE_BOTTOM` alone where it has no padding of its own
- A box floating over the page (a popover's or a menu's paper) fits a phone: a width wider than one is responsive (`width: { xs: "100%", sm: 560 }`, the popover's own gutters keeping it in), never a fixed `minWidth` that bleeds past the screen (`arkyvree/spacing`)
- A dialog is a `Modal` or a wrapper built on it (`FormDialog`, `CreateDialog`, `EditDialog`, `ConfirmDialog`, `DeleteDialog`), named `…Dialog` (`AddLevelDialog`; `Modal` is the wrapper's name alone, `arkyvree/component-files`), which goes full screen on a phone by itself (`fullScreen={isMobile}`): `Modal` is the one module that imports MUI's `Dialog` (`no-restricted-imports`), and a `Dialog` written anyway must go full screen too (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`). A full-screen dialog has no backdrop to tap, so every dialog needs its own way out: a Close or Cancel button
- Never use hover-only interactions without a touch-friendly fallback. A row's actions are a `RowActions` of `RowAction`s (`components/common`), at the row's end: a table's own last column (`<DataCell slot="trailing" align="right">` under its "Actions"), and the row spreads `ROW_ACTIONS_HOVER_SX` into its `sx` (`RulesetSectionTable`, `ContributorsTable`, `EquipmentTable`, the players', the requirement tree's cards, a class's levels in its popover on the sheet's header): revealed on hover with a pointer, always shown on touch screens and while focused. A `RowAction` is its icon, its `label` (its name and its tooltip) and its `intent`'s color: grey (Edit, Duplicate), red for what destroys (`"destructive"`: Delete, Remove, an action menu's "Delete …" and "Remove …" items too: `arkyvree/button-intents`), orange for leaving (`"caution"`: Leave, Revoke Invite), its icon rolling while the request it started runs (`pending`). A table that takes its rows' actions from its caller (`renderActions`) holds the `RowActions` itself, its caller handing `RowAction`s (`arkyvree/row-actions`). A row of a dialog's list shows its one action as a `RowAction` alone, always (a variant's Remove Variant, a Local Change's), never an icon button of its own

## Components

- A component is a `function` that destructures its props in its signature, typed by one named type: its own (`interface CardProps`, `type CardProps = Omit<…>`) or one its family shares (`RulesetSectionProps`), never written in place (`arkyvree/component-props`). React's types and functions are named imports (`import { type ReactNode } from "react"`), never `React.` or a default `React`, and a component takes its `ref` as a prop, never through `forwardRef` (`arkyvree/react-imports`)
- Every control has an accessible name. An icon-only `IconButton` is named by its `aria-label`, whether or not a `Tooltip` shows its name (`arkyvree/accessible-icon-buttons`). A `Tooltip` on an element its own text names (a chip, a list option, a labelled button) takes `describeChild`, otherwise its title replaces the element's name, and one around a control that can be disabled wraps it in a `<span>`, which still takes the pointer; no tooltip has an arrow, each keeping its own delay and placement; a tooltip's child takes focus, so it opens from the keyboard too, never an icon alone (`HelpLabel`'s icon is focusable); and a tooltip is the `Tooltip`, never the browser's `title` (the collapsed sidebar names its items with it; `arkyvree/tooltips`). Every control of the app bar names itself in a tooltip, its icon alone naming nothing (a phone's Open Navigation, Feedback, the notifications bell, the account menu). A switch or checkbox is the `control` of a `FormControlLabel`, and a dialog without a `DialogTitle` points `aria-labelledby` at its heading; one with a `DialogTitle` is named by it, which MUI wires (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`). A button that flips between two states keeps one label, naming what it turns on (`"Star Ruleset"`, `` `${label} Details` ``), and says which state it's in: `aria-pressed`, or `aria-expanded` when it shows or hides something, never a label that flips (`Star` / `Unstar`, `Show` / `Hide`; `arkyvree/toggle-states`)
- A button weighs what its action does ([ui-buttons.md](./ui-buttons.md)): a destructive, caution or positive one is contained, in its intent's color (Delete, Leave, Revoke Link, Proceed Anyway; `arkyvree/button-intents`), as a dialog's action is, and its way out is outlined. An intent is one type with one color map, `Intent` (`components/common/intent.ts`), which a menu's item (`ActionMenuItem`), a row's action (`RowAction`) and a dialog's action (`ConfirmDialog`'s and `DialogFooter`'s `intent`) take alike, never a color of their own. A roll of every die a step asks for is a labelled `RollAllButton` (`pages/characters`, beside `useDiceRoll`: a new character's ability scores, the levels' hit points; `arkyvree/roll-buttons`), one die's roll its dice icon, and the most a die gives a "Max" button beside it. A roll shows as its dice tumble and land through `useDiceRoll` (`pages/characters`), timed by `DICE_ROLL` (`theme/animations.ts`), never a timer of its own that rolls (`arkyvree/dice-rolls`)
- Everything a click opens is reachable from the keyboard. A row or card that opens or expands on click spreads `clickableProps(onActivate)` and puts `CLICKABLE_SX` in its `sx` (`components/common`): focusable, activated with Enter or Space, with a focus ring; a row puts `CLICKABLE_ROW_SX`, whose hover tint says it opens, and a row that doesn't open keeps its look: no row tints itself, with MUI's `hover` or an `"&:hover"` of its own; a bare `onClick` on a box, a row or a text is refused (`arkyvree/clickable-elements`). A control inside it (a link, a row action, a chip) keeps its click, and so does a menu or a dialog it opened, whose clicks reach the row through React: `clickableProps` leaves them be, so none stops its event (a control inside MUI's own toggles, an accordion's summary or a tree item, still does). What shows or hides something leads with one arrow, `ExpandArrow`, turned up while open: a heading's toggle is its `ToggleLabel` (`<SubsectionTitle><ToggleLabel open onToggle>Granted (3)</ToggleLabel></SubsectionTitle>`, its `label` naming the arrow alone when the heading holds a link: a feat's details on the sheet, "Dodge Details"), and a group the sheet opens and closes (a spell list, a feat's details, the granted feats) is that heading over a `Collapse`, never an outlined box, a table row's is `toggleProps(open, onToggle, "row")` with the arrow in its first cell, and an accordion's is its `expandIcon`, which the theme puts before its title; a link that opens a popover dialog ends with it, as a dropdown does (`LinkButton`'s `expanded`: a class's levels on a sheet's header) (`arkyvree/expand-arrows`). A control that only navigates is a link (`component={Link} to`; a page's Back, `DetailPageHeader`'s, `EntityDetailLayout`'s and `PageError`'s, takes where it goes, `backTo`), an external one an anchor (`component="a" href target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`), never `window.open`; React Router's `Link` is imported as `Link`, MUI's as `MuiLink` (`arkyvree/navigation`)
- An icon comes from `components/icons`, never from `@mui/icons-material` (`arkyvree/icons`): MUI's glyph under the name the app imports it by. Many keep the glyph's own name (`AddIcon`, `CloseIcon`), the others name what the icon means where it shows (`ContributorsIcon`, `UnassignedIcon`). One glyph per meaning, one meaning per glyph: the index exports each glyph once (`arkyvree/icons`), and a meaning takes its one name wherever it shows (modifiers `Tune`, contributors `Group`, players `Groups`, races `Face`, a fork `CallSplit`, a ruleset `MenuBook`, a character `Person`, spell uses `EventRepeat`, settings `Settings` (a ruleset's Settings tab, the account's), a class's levels `FormatListNumbered`, every removal the bin, `DeleteIcon`, a revoke `LinkOff`; a control named "Remove …" or "Delete …" shows the bin, which `arkyvree/icons` holds), a tab's and its empty state's alike. An icon the app lacks is added there, named for what it means, a glyph no other name holds
- What shows on a condition only is `cond && <X />`, never a ternary with a `null` side; a chain of alternatives (`a ? <A /> : b ? <B /> : null`) ends in null (`arkyvree/jsx-conditionals`). An element's attributes stand on consecutive lines, no blank line among them, which the formatter keeps (`arkyvree/jsx-attribute-lines`, `bun run lint --fix` closes them up). A fragment is `<>`, around two children or more (`react/jsx-fragments`, `react/jsx-no-useless-fragment`)
- A module sits in its domain's folder, beside what uses it: a component, a hook, a query (its area's `…Queries.ts`) or a helper only one area uses is that area's (`useFormOptions` in `pages/rulesets/hooks`, `linkQueries.ts` beside the sheet's fields section, `dice.ts` in `pages/characters`), and none is a base rules' alone: the client has no ruleset's folder. `components/common`, `hooks/` and `lib/` hold only what several areas share, and `lib/queries.ts` the queries several areas share (the sidebar's prefetches among them): one area's page and its prefetch share their area's (a card's hover), and a query a single module reads sits beside it (`oglLicenseQueries.ts` by `OglLicenseText`). A module holds what its name says, never a grab bag (`arkyvree/no-helpers-modules`), and a type lives with the code it describes: the component that owns it (`EditingLevel` in `TrackLevels.tsx`), or the query or the hook that gives it, derived from the API (`InferResponseType`, indexed access, `Pick`) or `shared/enums.ts`, never a `types/` folder nor a `types.ts` module, whatever it's prefixed with (`levelUpTypes.ts`); a `.d.ts` that declares ambient globals stays (`hooks/google.d.ts`, beside `useGoogleSignIn`; `appConfig.d.ts`, the shell's `AppConfig` from `shared/appConfig.ts`) (`arkyvree/no-types-modules`)
- Group related components in folders with `index.ts` exports. Code outside a folder imports it through its `index.ts`, never a file inside it; files within the folder (its subfolders included) import each other directly, and a subfolder with its own `index.ts` is imported through that. Code outside a component folder enters it at its outermost index, never a subfolder's (`components/characters/index.ts`, not `components/characters/sections/index.ts`; `arkyvree/folder-index`)
- An `index.ts` exports what code outside its folder uses. An entry, or a whole `index.ts`, that nothing imports is dead code: delete it. Page folders have none: routes import each page file directly
- `.oxlintrc.json` holds the client's imports: a page domain (`pages/rulesets/`, `pages/campaigns/`…) never imports another's code, so what two of them share lives in `components/` (`no-restricted-imports`, whose list of domains a new page folder joins); a shared folder's module (`components/`, `hooks/`, `contexts/`, `stores/`, `services/`, a page domain's `components/`, `hooks/` and `sections/`) exports by name, never by default (`import/no-default-export`); a component's module exports components, and constants, only, so Fast Refresh can reload it (`react/only-export-components`)

## Styles

The rules are `lint/styles.mjs`'s. Every value a style takes is the theme's, written out in `client/src/theme/` alone (`appTheme.ts`: the palette, the shadows it names, how MUI's components look, the app bar's too, which no `AppBar`'s `sx` repaints (`arkyvree/sx-conventions`), the app bar's height `TOOLBAR_HEIGHT`; `animations.ts`: durations, easings, keyframes and the animations they make; `globalStyles.ts`: the page's own, `GLOBAL_STYLES`, which `main.tsx` renders through `<GlobalStyles>`; `text.ts`: `lineClampSx`; `safeArea.ts`: the insets of the screen's edges the system draws over, `SAFE_BOTTOM` (the live inset in a browser's tab, Chrome's static one in an installed app: `BOTTOM_INSETS`, which `GLOBAL_STYLES` sets), `SAFE_LEFT`, `SAFE_RIGHT`, and `pastInset`; `errorTheme.ts`: `ERROR_THEME`, which the error page renders in, outside the app's providers, its title in `APP_FONT`, the app's typeface); everywhere else a style names one.

- A style is `sx`: never `styled()`, no stylesheet but the fonts `main.tsx` loads, and `style` only passes on one a component is given (`{ ...props.style, … }`); a table's column's width is its `TableColumn`'s (`width`), which its header's cell takes (`arkyvree/sx-styles`)
- A color is a palette token (`"text.secondary"`, `"gold.main"`, `theme.palette.backdrop.top`), translucent through `alpha()`: never a hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` color, a CSS color name or a hex alpha appended (`arkyvree/theme-colors`). The brand gold is `palette.gold`: `main` the theme's own (brighter on the dark theme), `light` and `dark` the two golds, `faint` its glow
- A shadow is an elevation (`boxShadow: 2`) or one the theme names: `theme.boxShadows.banner`, `theme.textShadows.hero`, `theme.dropShadows.authLogo` (`arkyvree/shadows`)
- A corner is in the theme's units (`borderRadius: 1`) or a circle (`"50%"`), and a corner of its own only drops to `0` beside it; spacing is in the theme's units (`p: 1.5`), never pixels; a layer above the page is the theme's (`theme.zIndex.drawer + 1`), a number orders siblings only (`0`, `1`) (`arkyvree/shape`). A border is the theme's shorthand (`border: 1`, `borderLeft: 3`), its color `borderColor`, its style `borderStyle` (`arkyvree/borders`)
- Motion is timed by `theme/animations.ts`: `ANIMATIONS.diceRoll`, `` `${fadeIn} ${DURATION.normal}ms ${EASING.decelerate}` ``, `transitionOf(["opacity"], DURATION.fast)`; keyframes and MUI's transitions (`Fade`, `Grow`…) are the theme's, and a component's timing props take its tokens (`<Collapse timeout={DURATION.normal}>`), never a number nor `"auto"`, and a `Collapse` always takes one, never MUI's default. The rest of the theme reads them too (a dialog's transition, a link's): only `animations.ts` writes a timing out. What animates, or moves on `&:hover`, stops for a viewer who asked for less motion: its style sets `[PREFERS_REDUCED_MOTION]` too (`arkyvree/motion`)
- Text takes its style from a variant (`variant="body2"`, a responsive one in `sx`: `typography: { xs: "body1", sm: "h6" }`), sized in `rem` when it sizes itself, in the theme's typeface (`APP_FONT`): a style's `fontFamily` is `"inherit"` or `"monospace"` alone; a font weight is a number; text aligns with `textAlign`, never `align`; an icon takes MUI's named sizes as its `fontSize` prop (`fontSize="small"`, other sizes in `sx`); lines clamp through `lineClampSx(lines)` (`arkyvree/type-scale`). A `Typography` sized as a heading or a subtitle declares its element (`component="h2"`, `"p"`), so the page's outline is a hierarchy (`arkyvree/headings`)
- A flex container is a `Stack`, its `direction` and `spacing` props (the theme makes `spacing` a gap: `useFlexGap`), never another element with a flex `display`; a grid is a `Box` (`display: "grid"`), never MUI's `Grid`; an element is a `Box` or a `Typography`, never a raw `div` or `p`; a spacer is `<Box sx={{ flexGrow: 1 }} />` (`arkyvree/flex-layout`). The gap between blocks is their `Stack`'s `spacing`, never a block's own margin (nor a field's `margin` prop): a margin only aligns (`mx: "auto"`) or resets (`0`), and nothing bleeds past its parent's padding: a table wider than a phone lists its rows there (`DataTable`), and scrolls sideways from `sm` up past its `minWidth`; a heading's gutter is `gutterBottom` (`arkyvree/spacing`). Padding is a box's inner space, never a gap between siblings: inside a surface (a background, a border, a shadow), an indent, a container's space from its own edges, or space that opens and closes with its content (inside a `Collapse`: `AnimatedAlert`'s `gutter`)
- `sx` is written one way (`arkyvree/sx-conventions`): MUI's shorthand keys (`bgcolor`, `p`, `mt`); an object whose values may read the theme (`color: (theme) => …`), never a function of its own; a style on a condition and a caller's `sx` join it as an array (`sx={[{ … }, open && { … }, ...(Array.isArray(sx) ? sx : [sx])]}`); a style wins by its selector, never `!important`, but on a third party's frame (`"& iframe"`), whose inline style no selector beats. The theme's mode is branched on as `theme.palette.mode === "dark"`, in the style it picks; MUI's `useTheme` and `useMediaQuery` are `useIsMobile`'s, and the app's theme mode is `useThemeMode`'s (`contexts/`)

## API Layer

- RPC client (`client/src/services/rpc.ts`) wraps Hono's `hc` client; its fetch throws `ApiError` (`client/src/services/ApiError.ts`) for typed error handling
- `ApiError` includes `status` (HTTP code), `errorName` (server error class name) and `issues` (a save's rules warnings, which `useValidationIssues` shows)
- The API is called through TanStack Query only: a request is made in a function a query or a mutation runs, named `…Fn` as TanStack's `queryFn` and `mutationFn` are, wherever it's handed (`useRulesetSection`'s `createFn`, an editor's `saveFn`, an entity page's `updateFn` and `removeFn`, a `const exportFn` handed to `usePdfExport`, a module's `function deleteEntityFn`; a `…Queries.ts` module's helpers are its queries'; `arkyvree/api-calls-in-queries`, which follows a part of `rpc` a variable holds). A request a click makes is a mutation, and a read it needs is `queryClient.fetchQuery`
- The RPC fetch throws `ApiError` on every non-2xx response, so never check `response.ok`. Read bodies with `parseResponse(rpc.api.x.$get(...))` (from `hono/client`), which also narrows to the success type, and a file's as a blob, `(await rpc.api.x.$get(...)).blob()` (`arkyvree/parsed-responses`). A path is written with dots where a segment is a name (`rpc.api.characters[":id"].share.$post`), brackets only for one that isn't (`[":id"]`, `["read-all"]`; `arkyvree/dot-notation`). To react to a specific status (e.g. show "not found"), catch `ApiError` and test `error.status`
- Use `InferRequestType` / `InferResponseType` from `hono/client` for all API types — never recreate manually: an endpoint is read off `typeof rpc` (`InferResponseType<(typeof rpc.api.rulesets)[":id"]["$get"], 200>`), and a type already declared is reused, never aliased (`type Option = Ability`)
- A query's options come from a `queryOptions` (or `infiniteQueryOptions`) factory in a `…Queries.ts` module, never written where it runs (`arkyvree/queries`): the caller does `useQuery(rulesetDetailQuery(id))`, or adds its own options (`{ ...entitiesQuery(rulesetId, kind, filters), placeholderData: keepPreviousData }`). Queries shared across areas live in `client/src/lib/queries.ts` (the lists and the dashboard's statistics, which the sidebar warms; a ruleset, `rulesetDetailQuery`, which a sheet's equipment reads too), so the key, page size and params can't drift apart; a page and the hover that prefetches it in its own area share its area's module (a campaign's card: `campaignDetailQuery`, `pages/campaigns/campaignQueries.ts`), and so does a query a single module reads, beside it (`oglLicenseQueries.ts`, `activityTargetQueries.ts`). A query keeps its previous data with `keepPreviousData`, its durations are named (`lib/durations.ts`: `FIVE_SECONDS`…), and the cache is written in a mutation's callbacks, or by the query's own module (`seedTargetPath`). An infinite query asks for its next page with `getNextPageParam: nextPage`, and its items are `pageItems(data)`, its first page `firstPage(data)` (`lib/pageItems.ts`), read as the component renders; only a value a hook's deps or another query's key read is memoized, on `data` (`useListboxQuery`'s `items`, which Add Level's class picker compares as it renders). Ruleset and campaign tab lists do the same in `pages/rulesets/details/sectionQueries.ts` and `pages/campaigns/details/sectionQueries.ts`, what a ruleset's pickers offer in `pages/rulesets/optionQueries.ts` (`entityOptionsQuery`, a kind's, and `templatesQuery`, a kind's templates of a type), whatever form or tab offers them, and so do the detail pages that a table row prefetches on hover: the customization page's entity in `pages/rulesets/customization/entityQueries.ts`, the simple entity pages (abilities, skills, saves…) in `pages/rulesets/details/entities/entityDetailQueries.ts`, and a progression owner's page and its tabs in `pages/rulesets/details/owners/ownerSectionQueries.ts`. A page renders with its factory and the hover prefetch (ruleset tab, campaign card, table row) goes through it. A kind's entities are read and written through the routes by kind: a kind's list and an entity are read by `lib/entityRequests.ts` (`readEntitiesFn`, `readEntityFn`, whose rows are `EntityRow`s), its other actions (`grouped`, `templates`, a create, an update, a delete) through the routes' own client. Every query key comes from `lib/queryKeys.ts` (`arkyvree/query-keys`: a key written as an array spreads one first), its words in camelCase (`spellsPerDay`)

## State Management

- **Server state**: TanStack React Query for all API data
- **Auth state**: Zustand store (`stores/authStore.ts`): who is signed in, and the emails waiting for a code. Only the user and `isAuthenticated` persist in localStorage (its `partialize`), and `checkSession` asks the server again. The requests that change it are `useAuthRequests`' mutations (sign in, up and out, start a demo, verify, reset; `pending` while any is in flight), and `checkSession` the private routes' probe (`components/auth/sessionCheck.ts`, which `PrivateRoute` runs once a tab); both sign the user in through the store's `startSession`, and the query client leaves their 401s to them. Where the user goes once the session changes is the routes' to say, never a page's: a signed-in user leaves the auth pages for where they were going (`AuthLayoutRoute`: the `redirect` that sent them to sign in, read back by `useAuthRedirect` and kept to this origin by `safeRedirectPath`), a signed-out one leaves the private pages for sign in (`PrivateRoute`, beside it in `components/auth`, its link built by `authPagePath`; `clearSession({ byUser: true })` when the user ended it, who isn't sent back). React Router applies a navigation in a transition, which the store's render runs ahead of: a page navigating as it changes the session loses to the route's redirect
- **Form state**: React Hook Form — use `form.reset()` to populate edit forms, never `key={}` remounting or `defaultValue={}`. A form is made with `useFormWith(defaultValues)` (`hooks/`), never `useForm` (`arkyvree/controlled-inputs`): its values are a whole `T`, every field the form's type requires, which TypeScript checks. A controlled field submits only what the form holds, so a field without a value would reach the server as missing. A ruleset entity's empty form is shared by its create dialog and its editor (`toEntityForm(columns)` in `pages/rulesets/components/forms/`, its rules' columns, none for a kind they give none; `EMPTY_MODIFIER` / `EMPTY_REQUIREMENT` in `components/customization`), its declared fields' empty values joined to it as the create opens (`withEntityFields(toEntityForm(columns), fields)`: its `createDefaults`). Map a nullable text column to `""` in the form values (`description: x.description ?? ""`): an input holds `""`, so `undefined` makes the form dirty on mount. A select's none is `""` too (`SelectField`'s `emptyLabel` stores it), and the API stores either as null; an empty form (`toEntityForm(columns)`, with its declared fields' empty values) holds every field its form shows. A field written in the user's event (what follows from a pick: the slot an item fills) is marked dirty, as its input would be: `setValue(name, value, { shouldDirty: true })`, so the form's Save and its close guard see it. A field's error shows where its binding puts it (`fieldState.error`; the shared fields say it under themselves), never read from `formState.errors` beside it. A rule the API checks on a field is the field's rule (a new feat's or spell's pools: `requiredRules(field.required)`, in the words its column's description gives), never a check thrown in `mutationFn`, which would say it in a toast (`arkyvree/controlled-inputs`). Every input is controlled, and a form's field is bound one way: through `useController`, by the shared fields (`NameField`, `DescriptionField`, `EmailField`, `PasswordField`, `FormTextField`, `TextAreaField`, `SelectField`, `SwitchField`; a code's `VerificationCodeField`, a character's `NotesField` and `PrivateNotesField`) or by `Controller` / `useController` around a custom input, never `register` and never a value `watch` reads with a `setValue` for its change (`arkyvree/controlled-inputs`). A custom input passes `field.ref` to the input element (`inputRef`), so a failed submit can focus it. An input component takes its value and its change from its owner and keeps no copy of the value; an editor with state of its own (`ConditionFields`' template mode) seeds it once as it mounts, in a dialog its form was reset for. A field the viewer can't edit is `readOnly` (`slotProps={{ input: { readOnly } }}`, `SelectField`'s `readOnly`): it reads as it is, never a disabled one restyled to look editable (`arkyvree/form-fields`). A page the viewer can't edit shows the same form, its fields read-only: no outline, no arrows or ×, the value at full contrast. Its form stands under `ReadOnlyFields` (`components/common`), which makes every shared field read-only (`FormTextField` and its presets, `SelectField`, `SwitchField`, an entity's declared fields) and a custom input that asks `useReadOnlyFields()` (an aptitudes picker, a level's saves and feats), gives them that look once, for all of them (each label above its value, as tall as they are, a multiline field without its minimum rows, the same gaps between fields as its editor's, a switch's or a checkbox's state muted, a default cursor, no hover or focus highlight), and each field says a value that isn't there as `EmptyValue` does, "—" (`EMPTY_VALUE_TEXT` where only text goes: a text's or a list's placeholder; a select's shown value), and its card shows no Save (`EntityDetailsCard`): the customization page's editors, a simple entity's page, a class's details and the Settings tab

## Hooks & Patterns

- `useDebouncedValue(value)` — shared hook for debouncing search inputs (300ms, one delay for every search); a cleared value applies at once, so resetting a search never filters by the old text
- An effect synchronizes with what's outside React (a subscription, a timer, the server through `useFormSync`), and never does what an event or a render does: it never writes a form's field, navigates (a redirect is a rendered `<Navigate>`) nor calls back its owner (an `on…` prop, a callback a ref holds; `arkyvree/effect-writes`). A change happens in the event that causes it (a picked target path seeds its operator in the pick, a picked item fills its slot as its details load), and what follows from data is derived as the component renders (a level's picks fitted to the pools its slots give, `fitPicks.ts`; a race its list no longer offers refused by the form's rules). A dialog's own state is reset by its opener (`form.reset`, the publish dialog's kind) or lives in a body the dialog unmounts as it closes (the extension picker's selection)
- A component's handler and what it derives are plain values, made as it renders (`const handleRowPrefetch = (row) => …`, a table's columns): `useCallback` and `useMemo` keep only what a hook's deps, a context's value (the toasts', `SnackbarProvider`) or another query's key read, a ref callback that sets state (the sidebar's), and what a `memo` component is handed (a skill's row in the level wizard), never what any other component is handed, which re-renders with its owner anyway (a section's row handlers, `RulesetSectionTable`'s columns), nor what a handler or the render reads. An Autocomplete whose `value` is built as it renders, which it answers with a `"reset"` that would clear its search as it's typed, holds its search itself (`inputValue`) and ignores that reason (`GrantsInput`, a level's feats; a character's languages), rather than memoizing the value
- `useOnChange(value, onChange)` (within `hooks/`: `useSearchText` uses it, and the index exports it once a component needs it) — follows a value from outside React's state into the state a component keeps, in the render that sees it change, never an effect that sets state (`react/set-state-in-effect`): `useSearchText`'s text takes the URL's at a navigation. Derive what can be derived during render instead
- `useRulesetSection` — generic CRUD hook for ruleset detail sections (queries, mutations, dialogs, forms); spread its `createDialogProps`, `editDialogProps` and `deleteDialogProps` into the section's `CreateDialog`, `EditDialog` and `EntityDeleteDialog`, overriding `onSubmit` after the spread when the section sends more than its form's values (a requirement's level). Its rows are its `query`'s (a customization tab's, a class's levels), or a paged list's `sectionName`, which its saves refresh; a row's duplicate opens its create dialog on the row's values, `handleDuplicate(row, values)`, which sends them through its `duplicateFn` (a modifier's, an item's), and a create opens on `createDefaults`, which a section may compute (a class's next level, `nextLevel`). A save hands what it answered to its `onCreateSuccess` (the created entity, whose page a list opens), `onUpdateSuccess` or `onDeleteSuccess`: a customization tab's follow a copy of an inherited entity through `followCopiesOf(entityId, followCopy)` (`pages/rulesets/followCopies.ts`: its `tag` marks a request with the entity it was sent for, and its `followCopies` are the three callbacks)
- `useRulesetPermissions(ruleset)` (`hooks/`) — the single source of ruleset edit / manage / publish rights on the client: the ruleset's pages, and a character sheet's Create Item, which shows to who may edit its ruleset's items
- `useFormSync(form, values, { key, updatedAt })` — keeps an inline edit form in step with server data without wiping unsaved edits on refetch; use it instead of an effect that calls `form.reset()` whenever the query data changes. Every inline form goes through it, one that edits nothing the server holds (a new password) given its empty values: its `sync.isDirty` enables its `SaveButton`, and while it's true a reload of the page asks first (it calls `useDirtyForm`; no form reads `formState.isDirty` of its own, `arkyvree/dirty-forms`). `key` is the record from the URL (include the ruleset id for ruleset entities: inherited ones keep their id in every fork), so opening another record resets the form. A ruleset entity's page follows a copy-on-write to the copy one way, `useCopyOnWrite(rulesetId, entityId, pathOf)` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`: its `followCopy`, onto the tab shown now, which a progression owner's Levels, Skills and customization tabs follow too, through `useOwnerCopy`, and its form's `key` and `adoptKey`, the source's key, which a form still holding the source takes over, keeping its unsaved edits; then `forgetSource` once the copy's data is in). Submit through `sync.handleSubmit`, read what the form is based on with `sync.baseline()` (the server values, which `useEntitySave` sends an edit's changed `fields` against), send `sync.updatedAt()` as the save's stale-edit token, and on success call `sync.saved(values, response.updatedAt)` instead of `form.reset()`: a ruleset entity's page does all of it through `useEntityForm` (its form on its empty values, its sync, and its save, `useEntitySave`, which nothing else calls: `arkyvree/entity-pages`), handing its card its `save` (its submit, `canSave` once something changed and the page isn't `locked`, `isSaving`)
- `useListParams(sortFields, defaultSort)` — a list page's search and sort from the URL, plus the `searchBarProps` that change them and `updateSearchParams`, which writes the page's other params: the search is the hook's state (`SearchBar` is controlled; the router applies a URL change in a transition, which can't feed a text input), written to the URL as it's typed and taking the URL's at Back or a link, and `search` is it debounced, for the query. `useSearchText(key)` does the same for a section's search. `useSearchParam` for any other filter, and a filter a list's tab offers is in the URL, never in a component's state (a ruleset list's pool, where its kind's `pools` say it narrows by one: `usePoolFilter(rulesetId, pools)`, at their `param` in the URL, the picked pool read back for its picker with its `poolError` (the picker's `loadError`); a list's tier, where its kind's `levels` say: `useTierFilter(baseRules, allowAll)`, both in `pages/rulesets/hooks`); a param is read through `oneOf()` (`lib/oneOf.ts`), never compared or converted raw (`=== "true"`, `Number(…)`). A picked filter, view or sort pushes a history entry, so Back returns to the one before; typed text replaces its entry (`useSearchText`, `useListParams`), so Back never steps through each letter. Sort options come from `NAME_SORTS` / `CREATED_SORTS` / `UPDATED_SORTS` (`CHANGED_SORTS` where a record's last change has a column of its own: a character's `lastChangedAt`, which its sheet's rows move)
- `useNotificationActions` — accept / reject invites, download exports and open targets for any notification surface. Every surface (the page, the dashboard's card, the bell's panel) shows a notification through `NotificationMessage` (`components/notifications`): its message, its details inline under it (a tooltip never opens on a phone), and, where read and unread ones list together, the unread dot (`unread`) with `UNREAD_NOTIFICATION_SX`'s tint on its row. What an activity or a notification changed shows inline under it through `ActivityDetails`, on the activities page too, never in a tooltip (`arkyvree/notification-messages`)
- `useAnswerInvite(onNoLongerPending?)` (`components/invites`) — accepts and rejects an invite of any kind (`inviteKinds.ts`' `INVITE_KINDS`, beside it: its requests, an accept's answering the record it gave rights on, which it refreshes, its name in a toast, the list it adds to, and where accepting lands, its `entityPath` or `listPath`, which `acceptedPath(kind, entityId)` picks), one way wherever it's answered: a notification's buttons (`useNotificationActions`, its types `INVITE_NOTIFICATION_TYPES` from `shared/activity.ts`, which Mark All as Read leaves unread on the server) and the invite's own page (`InvitePage`). Its toasts, what it refreshes, the answer it's sending (`answering`, an `InviteVerb`, which `useAnsweringInvites` beside it reads back by notification, wherever it was clicked) and an invite no longer pending (a 409: answered or revoked elsewhere) are its own
- `rulesetOptionsQuery(type, rulesetId)` (`lib/queries.ts`) — any kind's whole list a reference or an amounts input reads (3.5's saves: the first 100, the most one request returns; rulesets have far fewer), for pickers and columns; don't query them ad hoc. `useRulesetRules(rulesetId)` (`hooks/`) gives a ruleset's rules, which its texts read: its words, and its help (`help`: a customization tab's, its Settings', a condition's template's examples). A character's picked links (3.5's languages) are its rules' to offer: the sheet's fields section asks `characterLinkQuery(characterId, link, entityIds)` (`components/characters/sections/linkQueries.ts`) for each link its `fields` block names, with the picks its form holds, which answers what it has without picking it (shown fixed) and what one more pick may add, none once its picks fill the room its rules give them: the client works out none of it. A grants input's list (a class level's feats) is `entityOptionsQuery`'s (`pages/rulesets/optionQueries.ts`): a ruleset has hundreds, so it searches the server and pages in as the listbox scrolls; a customization page loads its form's lists beside its entity, by its kind's columns (`useFormOptions`, `pages/rulesets/hooks`)
- `useOpenEntity(rulesetId)` — opens a ruleset entity's page with the current list as its Back target; `entityPageState(location.state)` (`pages/rulesets/entityPageState.ts`) reads that target back, and `entityPageBack(location.state, fallback)` gives where Back goes and its name ("Back to Class" from a class's tab, "Back to Feat" from a feat's customization tab, its Modifiers' row, the entity named in its ruleset's words once the page has its ruleset, `baseRules`: "Back to Spell", else "Back to Ruleset")
- `useValidationIssues(fallback)` (`components/characters`, the sheet's equipment and the level wizard's) — a save the server can refuse with rules warnings: shows them in the form (with a force save), toasts anything else, `fallback` naming what failed
- `useToggleSet()` — expanded rows / open groups: `keys` (the set), `toggle(key)` one at a time, `clear()`
- `useDialogState<T>(initial?)` — a dialog opened for a record, or for nothing (`true`): `target`, `open`, `openWith`, `close`, `onExited`, and `key`, which opening it is; opened from the start on `initial` (the sheet's Add Level, as a new character arrives from its create dialog: `characterPageState`'s `openLevelUp`; its target is the track it plans, `{ trackId }`, or none, `{}`)
- `useAnchorMenu()` — a menu that opens from what was clicked: `anchorEl`, `open`, `openMenu` (the button's click), `closeMenu`, and `closeMenuAnd(then)` for an item's click (`arkyvree/anchor-menus`)
- Infinite listboxes: `useListboxQuery(options)` returns the loaded `items` and the listbox's `onScroll`; give the listbox `ScrollSafeListbox`. Several queries behind one listbox share a `createListboxScrollHandler([...])` (`lib/listboxScroll.ts`); flatten any other infinite query with `pageItems(data)` (`lib/pageItems.ts`)
- A query that can't run yet passes `skipToken` as its `queryFn`, in its factory (not `enabled: !!id`, which `arkyvree/queries` refuses); `enabled` is for plain on/off gates
- Untyped JSON (activity and notification payloads, stored state, a socket's messages: `isWsEvent`, `shared/webSocketEvents.ts`, the server's `WsEvent`) is read through `isRecord` (`shared/isRecord.ts`) guards, not casts, and whether a value is one of a list is `isOneOf(value, list)` (`shared/isOneOf.ts`), which narrows it, never a `.some` comparing each (`arkyvree/one-of`)
- What the browser keeps is a store's (`client/src/stores/`: zustand's `persist`, or a module of its own, `themeModeStorage.ts`), read and written through `browserStorage.ts`'s guards (`readStored`, `writeStored`, `removeStored`), which a blocked storage (a private window, blocked site data) never makes throw: never `localStorage` or `sessionStorage` elsewhere (`arkyvree/browser-storage`). The URL's search params are read through the shared hooks (`useSearchParam`, `useListParams`, `useSearchText`), never `useSearchParams` elsewhere, and a page's router state through its guard (`entityPageState`, `authPageState`, `characterPageState`), never a member of `location.state` (`arkyvree/navigation`). A date is shown through `lib/formatDate.ts` (`formatDate` and `formatDateTime` in the viewer's language; `formatRelativeTime`, "5m ago", in English, past a month its date and time), never formatted in place (`arkyvree/date-formats`)
- Mutations use `.mutate()` with `onSuccess`/`onError` callbacks, not `.mutateAsync()`, and a mutation is held whole (`const saveMutation = useMutation(…)`, `saveMutation.isPending`), never destructured (`arkyvree/client-apis`)

its list, `ValidationIssueList`, says the sheet's diagnostics' issues too, each message as the engine writes it, which names its entity in the ruleset's words: "Power Attack (Feat)") (no other alert carries actions: a confirmation is a `ConfirmDialog`, over a dialog too; `arkyvree/confirm-dialogs`). A select outside a form is a `TextField select`: `FormControl` + `InputLabel` + `Select` leaves the combobox without an accessible name. Ruleset tables use `DescriptionCell` and `ListValue` (a `pools` column's chips, `LinkChipsCell`: a row's links of the column's `role`, by their targets' names), and a list's filters `PoolAutocomplete` and `TierFilter` (`pages/rulesets/components`); the level-up wizard's options show what they are, or what they ask that the character lacks, through `OptionTooltip` (`arkyvree/option-tooltips`). A form is `noValidate`: its rules check its fields and say why, never the browser's bubble, so a bound field that asks the browser to check it (`required`, `type="email"`) has `rules` (an `EmailField` its own) and one with native bounds checks them with `wholeNumberRules` (`arkyvree/form-validation`). A form's fields stand 24px apart: the column holding them takes `spacing={3}`, a create or edit dialog's body as an entity page's editor (`EntityDetailsCard`), a profile's and an auth page's. Formatting helpers live in `lib/formatNumeric.ts` (`formatCount`, `formatCost`, `formatDecimal`, `formatDie`: a hit die's "d8", `formatWeight`, `formatTrimmed` and its `poundsUnit`: "1 lb", "2 lbs") and `lib/errorMessage.ts`, and a bonus's sign in `formatSigned` (`shared/text.ts`), while what the rules write (a sheet's attacks, critical and speed, its spell lists) comes written in the character's response, which the sheet shows as it is; an empty value is an `EmptyValue`. A page's content sits 32px under its header (`PageHeader`, `DetailPageHeader`, `CharacterHeader`): the column holding it takes `spacing={4}` (`arkyvree/page-gaps`). What the app says is said one way: a label (a button's, a menu item's, a toggle's, a tab's, a field's, a dialog's title, an icon button's tooltip, any `aria-label`, any component's `label` and `title` but what holds a sentence (a chip's value, an empty state's line; an alert's title is a label, "Validation Warnings"), a page's title, `usePageTitle`) is in Title Case, "Mark All as Read" (`arkyvree/label-case`); the app's name is `APP_NAME` (`lib/brand.ts`), never written out (`arkyvree/app-name`); an ellipsis is "…" and a dash between words "—", a template's interpolations reading as words (`` `${pool} — ${level}` ``), and a text cut short is `truncate(text, length)` (`lib/truncate.ts`), and a text says itself in words, never an emoji (a campaign's title too; `arkyvree/typography-marks`). Reuse them rather than restyling a copy.

## Toast/Snackbar

- Queue-based `SnackbarProvider` in `contexts/SnackbarProvider.tsx` — notifications process sequentially, each once the one before has closed; a closing toast whose transition never says its exit ended gives way a moment past its exit anyway (`TOAST_EXIT_WAIT`), so the queue can't hold every toast after it until the page reloads, and only the toast closing gives way (each has its `id`), so its late exit never drops the next one
- Use `useSnackbar()` hook: `snackbar.success()`, `snackbar.error()`, etc. `info` takes options: an `action` (`{ label, onClick }`, a button on the toast) and `persistent` (it stays until it's dismissed), as the new version's Refresh does (`WebSocketProvider`)
- Never use `console.error` for user-facing errors — always use snackbar. An error's toast names what failed, shown when the error has no message of its own: `snackbar.error(error, "Failed to archive campaign")` and `useValidationIssues("Failed to save item")` require it, and its words start "Failed to". A toast with no error caught isn't an error's: a warning or an info. An error shows through `errorMessage(error, fallback)`, never its raw `message`, and is told apart by its `status` or its `errorName`, never its words (`arkyvree/error-reads`). A server error's name is read once, by a predicate in `lib/errorMessage.ts` beside `accessLost(error)`: `emailNotVerified(error)`, and so is a status a predicate names, a 429 by `rateLimited(error)`
- A toast is a phrase: "Ruleset archived", "This export expired: generate a new one", no final period or "!", no "successfully" nor "Please", never "You have …", and an invite is an "invite", never an "invitation" (`arkyvree/toast-wording`)

## Loading Indicators

- Always use `<DiceSpinner>` (`components/common/DiceSpinner.tsx`) — never MUI `CircularProgress` (`arkyvree/client-apis`)
- For Buttons, use the wrapper API so the button doesn't shrink when loading flips: `<DiceSpinner size="small" loading={isPending}>Save</DiceSpinner>` as the Button child. Pair with `disabled={isPending}`. Keep any static `startIcon` outside the wrapper. Every button that starts a request shows it running this way, an icon button's icon too, and so does an action its owner hands a request to (Proceed Anyway's `pending`; `arkyvree/pending-buttons`). A component whose request is in flight says so by its `pending` (a dialog wrapper's, `DialogFooter`'s, `SaveButton`'s, `AuthSubmitButton`'s, `ActionMenuItem`'s): never `isLoading`, a query's first load (a table's skeleton, `EntityDetailLayout`'s), nor `loading`, which a spinner and MUI's picker say of themselves (`arkyvree/pending-props`).
- A page's first load, a route's `Suspense` fallback too, is a `PageLoader` (a character sheet's page draws the sheet's skeleton instead, `CharacterDetailSkeleton`). A section's or a dialog's loader is a standalone `<DiceSpinner />` (default medium); it centers itself, so give it a section's spacing through `sx` (`<DiceSpinner sx={{ py: 4 }} />`) and nothing else, never a box around it, a height or the large size. A menu's or a popover's loader takes the room of its one line (the bell's, in place of "No unread notifications"): the small die, given a line's spacing (`<DiceSpinner size="small" sx={{ py: 1.5 }} />`) (`arkyvree/page-loaders`)
- A table's first load is a `TableSkeleton`, its header over rows of skeletons, a list of lines on a phone (`RulesetSectionTable`'s, a campaign's players); anything else loads as a section's spinner, a grid's and a tree's too. A skeleton means loading, only: what's empty or hidden (a field its owner hides, a pick not made yet) says so in a `BlankNote` (`arkyvree/skeletons`)

## Dialog Conventions

- Pick the wrapper by purpose — there is no lint rule to choose between them, only a convention:
  - **`CreateDialog` / `EditDialog`** (from `StandardDialogs.tsx`): standard create / edit shapes. Default for create or update flows. Their submit waits (`submitDisabled`, its `DialogFooter` action's `disabled`) while what the form holds would be refused as it stands, as the API says it: an inventory slot its warning says is taken.
  - **`FormDialog`**: any other dialog that contains a React Hook Form. Binds the form, registers with the global dirty-tracker, blocks backdrop / Escape close while dirty.
  - **`ConfirmDialog` / `DeleteDialog`** (from `StandardDialogs.tsx`): yes/no confirmations, over a page or over the dialog that asks (the level wizard's discard, the share link's revoke). Set its `intent` (`caution` for Archive / Leave, `positive` for Publish, `destructive` for what destroys) and its `confirmLabel`, which it requires, to the action ("Archive Character", never "Confirm"). `DeleteDialog` is the `destructive` preset. A confirmation asks "Are you sure you want to …?", then says what follows; a deletion ends "This action cannot be undone.", or "You can restore it from Local Changes." for what a fork inherits, and an archive "You can unarchive it at any time from the Archived filter.", or "Only its owner can unarchive it." to who archives what they don't own (a ruleset's Admin) (`arkyvree/confirm-wording`): a ruleset's delete is an `EntityDeleteDialog` (`pages/rulesets/components`; an entity page's, its `EntityDetailLayout`'s own), which says which, and its row action and its page's menu say "Delete" or "Delete Permanently" alike (`useRestorableDelete`, `pages/rulesets/hooks`).
  - **`Modal`**: info, manager and picker dialogs that don't bind a form (Link Character to Campaign and Subscribe to Extensions hold their pick in their own state). **Never wrap a `<form>` in `Modal`** — Modal skips the dirty-close guard, so backdrop click / Escape discards typed input (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`). The level wizard (`LevelWizardDialog`) is the one `Modal` around a form, whose steps hold no `<form>`: it guards its own close, ignoring a backdrop click and asking before Cancel or Escape discards its progress (a `ConfirmDialog`): Add Level's once a class is planned, but the one a class's plus opened it with while the wizard hasn't moved on from its class plan, Edit Level's once a pick changed, as an edit form's dirty state says.
- A dialog is `sm`, its wrappers' default: a plain form (a name, a description, a select: Create New Class, Create New Mechanic, Create Variants), a picker (Subscribe to Extensions), a confirmation, a text (License & Attribution), a manager (both contributors'). It's `md` when its form has many fields in two columns (the spell's, a new character's) or it shows a target path or a requirement tree (a modifier's, a requirement's, an inventory item's, a character's Manage Modifiers, the level wizard), and never `xs`, which `arkyvree/dialog-conventions` refuses
- A dialog's lead line, the first thing it says, is a `DialogContentText` (body1, grey), as a confirmation's. Its content keeps the theme's padding, its title right above it (in its `<form>` when it has one), and its first block sits 8px under the title (`sx={{ pt: 1 }}` on the column it opens with), as a create dialog's fields (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`)
- Dialog title and action-bar styling come from the theme — use a plain `<DialogTitle>` of its words, no icon (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`), without extra typography or padding, and end the dialog with a `DialogFooter` (`onCancel`, its `cancelLabel` "Close" when there's nothing to confirm, an `action` with its `label`, `intent`, `icon`, `disabled` and `onClick` (a submit without), `pending` while it saves, and its own steps as children, before the action: the level wizard's Back, its Next and Finish being its `action`), never `DialogActions` by hand, and its action is contained, as every dialog's is (`arkyvree/dialog-footers`).
- Manager dialogs with both a list and an invite form: keep the outer `Modal` for the manager and put the form in a nested `FormDialog` opened from an Invite button.
- Edit dialogs receive a `form` prop (from React Hook Form) — parent calls `form.reset({ ...data })` before opening. A create dialog's form is reset as it opens too (`useRulesetSection`'s `handleCreate`), never after a save, so a cancelled one reopens empty
- A create dialog's title is "Create New …" ("Create New Language", "Create New Character"), an edit dialog's "Edit …"; a dialog that does something else is titled with its action ("Fork Ruleset"). Four create dialogs say it otherwise today: "Add Player", a character's "Add Modifier", "Add Item to Inventory" and "Create Requirement"; one titled "Add …" submits in its words ("Add Player", "Add Modifier", "Add Item") and toasts "… added", never the default "Create" (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`)
- A dialog stays mounted, its `open` showing it, so it fades out as it closes. A dialog about a record (a row, a level) opens through `useDialogState` (`dialog.openWith(row)`, `open={dialog.open}`, `dialog.target`), which keeps the record while it fades, never an open flag beside a record of its own; one that keeps state of its own (the level wizards) mounts with that record (`{dialog.target && …}`) and lets it go once faded (`onExited`), so it opens clean, keyed by its opening (`key={dialog.key}`), so one opened again as it fades out (a level wizard reopened right after its Finish) mounts afresh rather than keeping what it held (`arkyvree/dialog-mounts`)
- A dialog that sends a request closes in its mutation's `onSuccess`; when the request fails it stays open, its toast saying why, so the user can retry or cancel (a confirmation too: never closed in `onError` or `onSettled`, nor as it sends: `mutate(x, { onSuccess })`, `arkyvree/confirm-dialogs`)
- A form dialog doesn't receive the record it edits as a prop — its form state is the source of truth; a confirmation receives what it names (`RemoveContributorDialog`'s `contributor`)
- A dialog that holds a form never loses it to a stray click: `FormDialog` (which `CreateDialog` and `EditDialog` are built on) ignores a backdrop click or Escape while its form's values differ from its defaults (`valuesEqual`, so a value set without `shouldDirty` counts), and any close while it's `pending`; it registers an open, changed form with `useDirtyForm`, so a reload asks first. A form dialog is one of them, never a guard written again in `onClose`; one that prefills on open does it with `form.reset(values)`, which makes them its defaults

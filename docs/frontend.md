# Frontend

How the client (`client/src/`) is written. Read it before writing or changing client code: every pattern the client follows is here, with the component or hook that is its one way and the lint rule that holds it.

Lint holds what it can, with no exception (`bun run lint`: the `arkyvree/*` rules of `lint/frontend.mjs`, `lint/components.mjs`, `lint/controls.mjs`, `lint/styles.mjs`, `lint/wording.mjs` and `lint/feedback.mjs`, the import rules of `.oxlintrc.json`, and the repo's general ones in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)). What lint can't judge is decided here: which empty state a place takes, a framed or a bare table, which dialog wrapper. A look the app doesn't have yet is the owner's call: propose it, never invent one, and never flatten one place's look into another's unasked.

## Before finishing

- TypeScript passes: `bunx tsgo --noEmit -p .`
- `bun run lint` passes (`bun run lint --fix` writes what it can), and `bun run format:check` (`bunx oxfmt` the changed files, never a directory)
- The impacted tests pass: `bun run test:changed`, and `tests/client` for the client's pure modules
- A change users see is looked at: the screens it touches, at a phone's width (375px) and a desktop's, in both themes

## A page

- Named `XPage.tsx` under `pages/`, its default export `export default function XPage` (`arkyvree/component-files`): `XDetailsPage` for a record's own page (`RulesetDetailsPage`, `SkillDetailsPage`), and a page that shows a record another way is named for that way (`SharedCharacterPage`, `CampaignCharacterPage`, `CustomizationPage`). `App.tsx` imports it lazily, a chunk of its own, but the two pages a visit lands on, `SignInPage` and `DashboardPage`, which it imports eagerly
- It names the browser tab with `usePageTitle(title)` (a simple entity's through the shell it renders, `RulesetEntityDetail`), which the app's name follows (`APP_NAME`, `lib/brand.ts`)
- Its column is a `Container` of the app's page width, its default (`lg`, 1200px, where `AppMain` caps every page): the lists and the records, the dashboard, a shared sheet, the account and activity pages, an entity page (`EntityDetailLayout`'s) alike, and no `maxWidth` of its own but a narrower one's: `"md"` for the legal page, which reads as text, and `"sm"` for an invite's card (`InvitePage`); an auth page stands in `AuthPage`'s panel. A tab's content fills it (`SectionContent`). The theme pads a `Container` 32px at its top and its end (`MuiContainer`), on a phone too: a page starts 32px under the app bar, and its column's `sx` never sets its top, but an invite's, whose card stands alone, centred (`arkyvree/page-gaps`)
- It fades in through `PageTransition`, around its loaded content, every state an invite's page shows too: its loader and its error render outside it (`EntityDetailLayout` and the auth pages' `AuthPage` carry it for theirs), and nothing inside it fades in again
- It opens on its header, its content 32px below (`arkyvree/page-gaps`): `PageHeader` for a list, an account, an activity or the legal page (`variant="tinted"` on the content lists, the rulesets, characters and campaigns; its default `hero` on the others), `DetailPageHeader` for a ruleset or a campaign, `CharacterHeader` for a character's sheet, `EntityDetailLayout` for a ruleset entity's page (a simple entity's, a class's, a customization's), which renders `DetailPageHeader` (a Back with nowhere to go yet hides: no `backTo`). Three pages open on a header of their own: the dashboard on its hero banner (`DashboardPage`), the auth pages on `AuthPage`'s title (`components/auth/AuthPage.tsx`; on a phone the layout's panel frames it under the brand, and it draws none of its own), an invite on its card (`InvitePage`, `pages/invites`, which each invite's route renders with its `kind`). Its title, its `h1`, is its header's alone (`arkyvree/page-titles`)
- It shows `PageLoader` while it first loads (a character sheet's page, the character's, a campaign's or a share link's, draws `CharacterDetailSkeleton` instead, `arkyvree/page-loaders`, and `EntityDetailLayout`'s `isLoading` a skeleton of itself), and `PageError` when it fails with nothing loaded to keep (`EntityPageError` on an entity page: a `PageError` in `EntityDetailLayout`'s column), never an alert of its own: its Back is named for where it goes, "Back to Campaigns" (`arkyvree/page-errors`). An entity page goes back to the list it was opened from, a ruleset's tab or a class's: `entityPageBack(location.state, fallback)` gives both the place and its name, its fallback what holds the entity (a class level's class, a modifier's entity) or its list, and its error goes back the same way (`arkyvree/entity-pages`). A part of a page that fails on its own shows a `LoadError` where it shows (a profile's linked accounts, the legal page's license, the dashboard's statistics). An archived record's page (a ruleset's, a campaign's, a character's) says so above its tabs or its sheet with `ArchivedNotice` (`what`, and `canUnarchive` for whom its menu offers Unarchive)

## Recipes

How the app's recurring screens are put together. Build a new one the same way, from the same pieces.

- **A list page** (the rulesets, characters, campaigns, notifications, activity): its search and sort come from `useListParams(sortFields, defaultSort)`, its other filters from the URL too (`updateSearchParams`, read back through `oneOf`), and its list from `useListPageQuery(xListQuery(filters))`: `lib/queries.ts`'s for the lists the sidebar warms (`rulesetListQuery`, `characterListQuery`, `campaignListQuery`), its page's own `…Queries.ts` for the others (`activityQueries.ts`, `notificationQueries.ts`). It opens on its list's defaults, beside its query (`RULESET_LIST_DEFAULTS`, `CHARACTER_LIST_DEFAULTS`, `CAMPAIGN_LIST_DEFAULTS`), the URL's when it says none, which the sidebar warms too. Under its `PageHeader` stand its `SearchBar` and its `ListPageResults`, a column of their own (`spacing={3}`): the first load, the failure while nothing has loaded, `NoMatchesState` for a search, its own `empty` state (a `BlankState`), else its grid (`ListCardGrid`) or its table (`TableFrame`), and a large Load More; a tab's list that isn't a ruleset table (a campaign's characters and players, a ruleset's feats grouped by family) shows the same way, `variant="section"`: a section's spinner, or its table's `skeleton`, and a medium Load More. A searched list's results show through it or a ruleset tab's `RulesetSectionTable` alone, which say a search found nothing (`NoMatchesState`): no other module draws one (`arkyvree/list-results`). A card is a `ListCard`, staggered in from the query's `offset` (`animationIndex`, `animationOffset`); a character's shows its race and classes through `CharacterBuildChips` (`components/characters`), and a list of characters its portraits through `useCharacterPortraits`
- **A record page with tabs** (`RulesetDetailsPage`, `CampaignDetailsPage`, `ClassDetailsPage`, `CustomizationPage`): its tab is the URL's `:section` segment (`rulesets/:id/:section` in `App.tsx`), and a URL without a known one renders a `<Navigate … replace />` to its first tab. `SectionTabs` warm a tab's data as it's hovered (`onTabHover`: the ruleset's `prefetchSection`, the class's `prefetchClassSection`), and its content is a `SectionTabPanel`, 56px under them (`arkyvree/page-gaps`). Its actions are its header's menu: `DetailPageHeader`'s `onMenuOpen` is `menu.openMenu` (`useAnchorMenu()`) when the session has something to do there, else `undefined`, which hides the button; each item is an `ActionMenuItem` (an action's `onClick={menu.closeMenuAnd(() => …)}`, a page of the app's `to` or an outside page's `href` with `onClick={menu.closeMenu}`, `pending` while the request it started runs). The ruleset page keeps its dialogs, forms and mutations in an operations hook, `useRulesetOperations` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`), as the campaign list keeps its create in `useCampaignOperations` (`pages/campaigns/hooks`); the other record pages hold theirs. What the session may do comes from `useRulesetPermissions(ruleset)`, `useCampaignPermissions(campaign)` or `useCharacterPermissions(character)` (`pages/characters/details`): an action shows only when it's allowed, and a dialog's or a notice's rights are its too, never worked out where they're passed (`ContributorsDialog`'s `canInvite`, `canLeave` and `canRemove`, `ArchivedNotice`'s `canUnarchive`)
- **A ruleset section tab** (`pages/rulesets/details/sections/`, `LanguagesSection`): a component of `RulesetSectionProps` (`ruleset`, `childOnly`, `onChildOnlyChange`; `pages/rulesets/details/sectionFactory.ts`) in a `SectionContent`. Its columns are a module constant (`LANGUAGES_COLUMNS`: `key`, `label`, `width`), its search `useSearchText("search")`, its list an infinite query of `sectionQueries.ts` (`languagesQuery(ruleset.id, { search, childOnly })`) read with `pageItems`, and its create `useRulesetSection`'s (below), its create dialog `<CreateDialog {...createDialogProps} title="Create New Language">` around its form fields; an entity is edited and deleted on its own page. Its toolbar is its `SearchBar`, `SectionActions` as its `actions` (Local Changes on a fork, the add button for an editor); its table a `RulesetSectionTable` (`what`, `error`, `search`, `columns`, `renderCell`; `onRowClick` opens the entity through `useOpenEntity`, `onRowMouseEnter` warms its page (`useRowPrefetch`); `onEdit`, `onDuplicate` and `onDelete` with `canEdit` give a row its actions, where a table has them: the items' Duplicate, a customization tab's Edit and Delete); then a `LoadMoreButton`
- **A ruleset entity's form** (`pages/rulesets/components/forms/`, 3.5's in `forms/dnd3.5/`): three pieces per entity, which its create dialog and its page share: `XFormData`, its request's body (`InferRequestType<…["$post"]>["json"]`; an item's holds its cost and its weight as their fields' text, which `toItemPayload` sends as numbers), `XFormFields({ form })`, its fields bound to the form, and `EMPTY_X` (`emptyForms.ts`), the form's empty values. What customizes an entity (a property, a modifier, a requirement) has its pieces in `components/customization`, beside its inputs (`PropertyFormFields`, `EMPTY_PROPERTY`)
- **A simple entity's page** (abilities, aptitudes, languages, mechanics, saves, skills; `pages/rulesets/details/entities/`): a `RulesetEntityDetail` given its `section`, `label`, `query` (`entityDetailQueries.ts`) and `renderChips`, and, when it can be edited, its `editing`: `empty`, `toFormValues`, `renderFields`, `updateFn`, `removeFn`. It lays the page out in `EntityDetailLayout`, shows an `EntityDetailsCard` (the form for an editor, else the description; its `renderChips` gives chips alone, and what failed to load for the description is its `notice`, stated above it: `arkyvree/load-errors`), follows a save of an inherited entity to its copy, and fails into `EntityPageError`. A skill's is its base rules' (`SkillDetails`, `details/entities/dnd3.5/`), which its page renders through `getEntityPages` once the ruleset is in. An entity page's header says what the entity is and where, under its name: `EntityDetailLayout`'s `what` and `rulesetName` ("Language in Core SRD 3.5"), a modifier's its target path instead (`subtitle`). An entity page (a simple entity's, a class's, a customization's) saves its details through `useEntitySave` and deletes through `EntityDetailLayout`'s `deletion` (`pages/rulesets`, below; `arkyvree/entity-pages`)
- **What a ruleset's base rules choose**: a table by base rules (`Record<BaseRules, …>`), read through a factory: the ruleset page's tabs and the license a system ruleset's header shows (`getSections`, `pages/rulesets/details/sectionFactory.ts`), an entity's page (`getEntityPages`, `pages/rulesets/details/entities/entityPageFactory.ts`: a skill's details), the customization page's editors (`renderEditor`, `pages/rulesets/customization/editors`: an item's, a power's, a class level's), the kinds the Races and Classes tabs filter by (`useEntityFilters(baseRules)`), a sheet's sections (`getSections`, `components/characters/sectionFactory.ts`), the level wizard's steps (`getLevelUpSections`, `pages/characters/details/components/levelUpFactory.ts`, which the Add Level and Edit Level dialogs render with). A factory sits above its base rules' folder (`dnd3.5/`), whose components declare their own props (`SpellsSectionProps` in `SpellsSection.tsx`), which its map is typed by (the ruleset page's tabs all take `RulesetSectionProps`); a key names its slot as the schema does and its base rules' component its own way (`PowersSection: SpellsSection`, `PowersStep: SpellsStep`: a 3.5 power is a spell), and the hooks that drive the screens give state, never the components. Its words and orders are tables by base rules too: `entityTypeLabel(entityType, baseRules, many?)` (`lib/rulesetLabels.ts`: a 3.5 power is a "Spell") and `sortAbilities(items, baseRules, getName)` (`components/characters/sections/abilityOrder.ts`). Another base rules adds its row to each
- **Prefetching**: what a user is about to open is loaded as they point at it, through the factory its page renders with: a card on hover and focus (`ListCard`'s `onMouseEnter` and `onFocus`), a table's row through `useRowPrefetch` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`: 150ms into a hover, at once on focus), which `RulesetSectionTable`'s `onRowMouseEnter` and the feats' grouped rows take, every row that opens a page giving one (`arkyvree/row-prefetch`), a fork's chip its parent's page (`RulesetFactChips` warms it itself), a tab through `SectionTabs`' `onTabHover`, and the sidebar's links on hover (`PREFETCHERS`, `components/layout/Layout.tsx`: a list's first page, under its `…_LIST_DEFAULTS`)
- **A mutation and the cache**: the query client (`lib/queryClient.ts`) never retries, keeps data fresh for `FIVE_MINUTES`, clears the cache when the signed-in user changes, and signs the session out on a 401 (an auth request's aside). A record's keys nest under its own (`QUERY_KEYS.rulesets.section(id, section)` is `["rulesets", "detail", id, section]`), so invalidating a key refreshes what's under it. A mutation's `onSuccess` says it in a toast, invalidates through its domain's helper, and closes its dialog: `invalidateRulesetEdit(queryClient, rulesetId, keys)` (`pages/rulesets/details/sectionQueries.ts`: the lists and the ruleset's Local Changes), `invalidateCharacter` (a character's sheet is invalidated through it alone, which refreshes its level-up too: `arkyvree/query-keys`) and `invalidateCharacterListings` (`lib/queries.ts`), `invalidateCampaignPlayers` (`pages/campaigns/details/sectionQueries.ts`); its `onError` is `snackbar.error(error, "Failed to …")`. A delete navigates away, then removes its record's queries (`queryClient.removeQueries`), so Back never renders it from the cache (`EntityDetailLayout`'s `deletion`, `CampaignDetailsPage`, `CharacterDetailsPage`). Data that never goes stale on its own takes `staleTime: FOREVER` (`lib/durations.ts`)
- **Staggered entry**: a list's items fade up one after another, `fadeInUpSx(index, offset)` (`theme/animations.ts`) in an item's `sx` (`ListCard`'s `animationIndex` and `animationOffset`, `RulesetSectionTable`'s rows and its `animationOffset`). A list that loads its pages animates only the page that came in: from `useListPageQuery`'s `offset` (`useStaggerAnimation`), or `itemsBeforeLastPage(data)` (`lib/pageItems.ts`) for another infinite query, a paged ruleset table's `animationOffset` (`arkyvree/list-results`)
- **Server events**: the server's events refresh the queries they change: `WebSocketProvider` (`contexts/`) invalidates the keys `EVENT_INVALIDATION_MAP` lists for an event (`notifications:updated`, `activities:updated`), where a new event's keys go

## Mobile First

- Develop mobile first — all new components and layouts must work on small screens (375px) before scaling up
- Use `useIsMobile()` hook (`client/src/hooks/useIsMobile.ts`) for mobile-specific branching: true below MUI's `md` breakpoint (900px), a tablet held upright included
- Use MUI responsive sx props (`{ xs: ..., sm: ..., md: ... }`) over static values
- A box floating over the page (a popover's or a menu's paper) fits a phone: a width wider than one is responsive (`width: { xs: "100%", sm: 560 }`, the popover's own gutters keeping it in), never a fixed `minWidth` that bleeds past the screen (`arkyvree/spacing`)
- A dialog is a `Modal` or a wrapper built on it (`FormDialog`, `CreateDialog`, `EditDialog`, `ConfirmDialog`, `DeleteDialog`), named `…Dialog` (`AddLevelDialog`; `Modal` is the wrapper's name alone, `arkyvree/component-files`), which goes full screen on a phone by itself (`fullScreen={isMobile}`): `Modal` is the one module that imports MUI's `Dialog` (`no-restricted-imports`), and a `Dialog` written anyway must go full screen too (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`). A full-screen dialog has no backdrop to tap, so every dialog needs its own way out: a Close or Cancel button
- Never use hover-only interactions without a touch-friendly fallback. A row's actions are a `RowActions` of `RowAction`s (`components/common`), at the row's end: a table's own last column (`<TableCell align="right">` under its "Actions"), and the row spreads `ROW_ACTIONS_HOVER_SX` into its `sx` (`RulesetSectionTable`, `ContributorsTable`, `EquipmentTable`, the players', the requirement tree's cards, the sheet's class levels): revealed on hover with a pointer, always shown on touch screens and while focused. A `RowAction` is its icon, its `label` (its name and its tooltip) and its `intent`'s color: grey (Edit, Duplicate), red for what destroys (`"destructive"`: Delete, Remove, an action menu's "Delete …" and "Remove …" items too: `arkyvree/button-intents`), orange for leaving (`"caution"`: Leave, Revoke Invite), its icon rolling while the request it started runs (`pending`). A table that takes its rows' actions from its caller (`renderActions`) holds the `RowActions` itself, its caller handing `RowAction`s (`arkyvree/row-actions`). A row of a dialog's list shows its one action as a `RowAction` alone, always (a variant's Remove Variant, a Local Change's), never an icon button of its own

## Components

- A component is a `function` that destructures its props in its signature, typed by one named type: its own (`interface CardProps`, `type CardProps = Omit<…>`) or one its family shares (`RulesetSectionProps`), never written in place (`arkyvree/component-props`). React's types and functions are named imports (`import { type ReactNode } from "react"`), never `React.` or a default `React`, and a component takes its `ref` as a prop, never through `forwardRef` (`arkyvree/react-imports`)
- Every control has an accessible name. An icon-only `IconButton` is named by its `aria-label`, whether or not a `Tooltip` shows its name (`arkyvree/accessible-icon-buttons`). A `Tooltip` on an element its own text names (a chip, a list option, a labelled button) takes `describeChild`, otherwise its title replaces the element's name, and one around a control that can be disabled wraps it in a `<span>`, which still takes the pointer; no tooltip has an arrow, each keeping its own delay and placement; a tooltip's child takes focus, so it opens from the keyboard too, never an icon alone (`HelpLabel`'s icon is focusable); and a tooltip is the `Tooltip`, never the browser's `title` (the collapsed sidebar names its items with it; `arkyvree/tooltips`). Every control of the app bar names itself in a tooltip, its icon alone naming nothing (a phone's Open Navigation, Feedback, the notifications bell, the account menu). A switch or checkbox is the `control` of a `FormControlLabel`, and a dialog without a `DialogTitle` points `aria-labelledby` at its heading; one with a `DialogTitle` is named by it, which MUI wires (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`). A button that flips between two states keeps one label, naming what it turns on (`"Star Ruleset"`, `` `${label} Details` ``), and says which state it's in: `aria-pressed`, or `aria-expanded` when it shows or hides something, never a label that flips (`Star` / `Unstar`, `Show` / `Hide`; `arkyvree/toggle-states`)
- A button weighs what its action does ([ui-buttons.md](./ui-buttons.md)): a destructive, caution or positive one is contained, in its intent's color (Delete, Leave, Revoke Link, Proceed Anyway; `arkyvree/button-intents`), as a dialog's action is, and its way out is outlined. An intent is one type with one color map, `Intent` (`components/common/intent.ts`), which a menu's item (`ActionMenuItem`), a row's action (`RowAction`) and a dialog's action (`ConfirmDialog`'s and `DialogFooter`'s `intent`) take alike, never a color of their own. A roll of every die a step asks for is a labelled `RollAllButton` (`pages/characters`, beside `useDiceRoll`: a new character's ability scores, the levels' hit points; `arkyvree/roll-buttons`), one die's roll its dice icon, and the most a die gives a "Max" button beside it. A roll shows as its dice tumble and land through `useDiceRoll` (`pages/characters`), timed by `DICE_ROLL` (`theme/animations.ts`), never a timer of its own that rolls (`arkyvree/dice-rolls`)
- Everything a click opens is reachable from the keyboard. A row or card that opens or expands on click spreads `clickableProps(onActivate)` and puts `CLICKABLE_SX` in its `sx` (`components/common`): focusable, activated with Enter or Space, with a focus ring; a row puts `CLICKABLE_ROW_SX`, whose hover tint says it opens, and a row that doesn't open keeps its look: no row tints itself, with MUI's `hover` or an `"&:hover"` of its own; a bare `onClick` on a box, a row or a text is refused (`arkyvree/clickable-elements`). A control inside it (a link, a row action, a chip) keeps its click, and so does a menu or a dialog it opened, whose clicks reach the row through React: `clickableProps` leaves them be, so none stops its event (a control inside MUI's own toggles, an accordion's summary or a tree item, still does). What shows or hides something leads with one arrow, `ExpandArrow`, turned up while open: a heading's toggle is its `ToggleLabel` (`<SubsectionTitle><ToggleLabel open onToggle>Granted (3)</ToggleLabel></SubsectionTitle>`, its `label` naming the arrow alone when the heading holds a link: a class's levels on the sheet, "Wizard Levels"), and a group the sheet opens and closes (a class's levels, a spell list, the granted feats) is that heading over a `Collapse`, never an outlined box, a table row's is `toggleProps(open, onToggle, "row")` with the arrow in its first cell, and an accordion's is its `expandIcon`, which the theme puts before its title (`arkyvree/expand-arrows`). A control that only navigates is a link (`component={Link} to`; a page's Back, `DetailPageHeader`'s, `EntityDetailLayout`'s and `PageError`'s, takes where it goes, `backTo`), an external one an anchor (`component="a" href target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`), never `window.open`; React Router's `Link` is imported as `Link`, MUI's as `MuiLink` (`arkyvree/navigation`)
- An icon comes from `components/icons`, never from `@mui/icons-material` (`arkyvree/icons`): MUI's glyph under the name the app imports it by. Many keep the glyph's own name (`AddIcon`, `CloseIcon`), the others name what the icon means where it shows (`ContributorsIcon`, `UnassignedIcon`). One glyph per meaning, one meaning per glyph: the index exports each glyph once (`arkyvree/icons`), and a meaning takes its one name wherever it shows (modifiers `Tune`, contributors `Group`, players `Groups`, races `Face`, a fork `CallSplit`, a ruleset `MenuBook`, a character `Person`, spell uses `EventRepeat`, properties `Label`, a class's levels `FormatListNumbered`, every removal the bin, `DeleteIcon`, a revoke `LinkOff`; a control named "Remove …" or "Delete …" shows the bin, which `arkyvree/icons` holds), a tab's and its empty state's alike. An icon the app lacks is added there, named for what it means, a glyph no other name holds
- What shows on a condition only is `cond && <X />`, never a ternary with a `null` side; a chain of alternatives (`a ? <A /> : b ? <B /> : null`) ends in null (`arkyvree/jsx-conditionals`). An element's attributes stand on consecutive lines, no blank line among them, which the formatter keeps (`arkyvree/jsx-attribute-lines`, `bun run lint --fix` closes them up). A fragment is `<>`, around two children or more (`react/jsx-fragments`, `react/jsx-no-useless-fragment`)
- A module sits in its domain's folder, beside what uses it: a component, a hook, a query (its area's `…Queries.ts`) or a helper only one area uses is that area's (`useRulesetSaves` in `pages/rulesets/hooks`, `useRulesetLanguages` beside the sheet's identity section, `dice.ts` in `pages/characters`), and one only a base rules uses sits under its `dnd3.5/` (`components/characters/sections/dnd3.5/`, `pages/rulesets/hooks/dnd3.5/`). `components/common`, `hooks/` and `lib/` hold only what several areas share, and `lib/queries.ts` the queries several areas share (the sidebar's prefetches among them): one area's page and its prefetch share their area's (a card's hover), and a query a single module reads sits beside it (`oglLicenseQueries.ts` by `OglLicenseText`). A module holds what its name says, never a grab bag (`arkyvree/no-helpers-modules`), and a type lives with the code it describes: the component that owns it (`EditingLevel` in `ClassesSection.tsx`), or the query or the hook that gives it, derived from the API (`InferResponseType`, indexed access, `Pick`) or `shared/enums.ts`, never a `types/` folder nor a `types.ts` module, whatever it's prefixed with (`levelUpTypes.ts`); a `.d.ts` that declares ambient globals stays (`hooks/google.d.ts`, beside `useGoogleSignIn`; `appConfig.d.ts`, the shell's `AppConfig` from `shared/appConfig.ts`) (`arkyvree/no-types-modules`)
- Group related components in folders with `index.ts` exports. Code outside a folder imports it through its `index.ts`, never a file inside it; files within the folder (its subfolders included) import each other directly, and a subfolder with its own `index.ts` is imported through that. Code outside a component folder enters it at its outermost index, never a subfolder's (`components/characters/index.ts`, not `components/characters/sections/dnd3.5/index.ts`; `arkyvree/folder-index`)
- An `index.ts` exports what code outside its folder uses. An entry, or a whole `index.ts`, that nothing imports is dead code: delete it. Page folders have none: routes import each page file directly
- `.oxlintrc.json` holds the client's imports: a page domain (`pages/rulesets/`, `pages/campaigns/`…) never imports another's code, so what two of them share lives in `components/` (`no-restricted-imports`, whose list of domains a new page folder joins); a shared folder's module (`components/`, `hooks/`, `contexts/`, `stores/`, `services/`, a page domain's `components/`, `hooks/` and `sections/`) exports by name, never by default (`import/no-default-export`); a component's module exports components, and constants, only, so Fast Refresh can reload it (`react/only-export-components`)

## Styles

The rules are `lint/styles.mjs`'s. Every value a style takes is the theme's, written out in `client/src/theme/` alone (`appTheme.ts`: the palette, the shadows it names, how MUI's components look, the app bar's too, which no `AppBar`'s `sx` repaints (`arkyvree/sx-conventions`), the app bar's height `TOOLBAR_HEIGHT`; `animations.ts`: durations, easings, keyframes and the animations they make; `globalStyles.ts`: the page's own, `GLOBAL_STYLES`, which `main.tsx` renders through `<GlobalStyles>`; `text.ts`: `lineClampSx`; `errorTheme.ts`: `ERROR_THEME`, which the error page renders in, outside the app's providers, its title in `APP_FONT`, the app's typeface); everywhere else a style names one.

- A style is `sx`: never `styled()`, no stylesheet but the fonts `main.tsx` loads, and `style` only passes on one a component is given (`{ ...props.style, … }`); a table's column is a `<Box component="col" sx={{ width }} />` (`arkyvree/sx-styles`)
- A color is a palette token (`"text.secondary"`, `"gold.main"`, `theme.palette.backdrop.top`), translucent through `alpha()`: never a hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` color, a CSS color name or a hex alpha appended (`arkyvree/theme-colors`). The brand gold is `palette.gold`: `main` the theme's own (brighter on the dark theme), `light` and `dark` the two golds, `faint` its glow
- A shadow is an elevation (`boxShadow: 2`) or one the theme names: `theme.boxShadows.banner`, `theme.textShadows.hero`, `theme.dropShadows.authLogo` (`arkyvree/shadows`)
- A corner is in the theme's units (`borderRadius: 1`) or a circle (`"50%"`), and a corner of its own only drops to `0` beside it; spacing is in the theme's units (`p: 1.5`), never pixels; a layer above the page is the theme's (`theme.zIndex.drawer + 1`), a number orders siblings only (`0`, `1`) (`arkyvree/shape`). A border is the theme's shorthand (`border: 1`, `borderLeft: 3`), its color `borderColor`, its style `borderStyle` (`arkyvree/borders`)
- Motion is timed by `theme/animations.ts`: `ANIMATIONS.diceRoll`, `` `${fadeIn} ${DURATION.normal}ms ${EASING.decelerate}` ``, `transitionOf(["opacity"], DURATION.fast)`; keyframes and MUI's transitions (`Fade`, `Grow`…) are the theme's, and a component's timing props take its tokens (`<Collapse timeout={DURATION.normal}>`), never a number nor `"auto"`, and a `Collapse` always takes one, never MUI's default. The rest of the theme reads them too (a dialog's transition, a link's): only `animations.ts` writes a timing out. What animates, or moves on `&:hover`, stops for a viewer who asked for less motion: its style sets `[PREFERS_REDUCED_MOTION]` too (`arkyvree/motion`)
- Text takes its style from a variant (`variant="body2"`, a responsive one in `sx`: `typography: { xs: "body1", sm: "h6" }`), sized in `rem` when it sizes itself, in the theme's typeface (`APP_FONT`): a style's `fontFamily` is `"inherit"` or `"monospace"` alone; a font weight is a number; text aligns with `textAlign`, never `align`; an icon takes MUI's named sizes as its `fontSize` prop (`fontSize="small"`, other sizes in `sx`); lines clamp through `lineClampSx(lines)` (`arkyvree/type-scale`). A `Typography` sized as a heading or a subtitle declares its element (`component="h2"`, `"p"`), so the page's outline is a hierarchy (`arkyvree/headings`)
- A flex container is a `Stack`, its `direction` and `spacing` props (the theme makes `spacing` a gap: `useFlexGap`), never another element with a flex `display`; a grid is a `Box` (`display: "grid"`), never MUI's `Grid`; an element is a `Box` or a `Typography`, never a raw `div` or `p`; a spacer is `<Box sx={{ flexGrow: 1 }} />` (`arkyvree/flex-layout`). The gap between blocks is their `Stack`'s `spacing`, never a block's own margin (nor a field's `margin` prop): a margin only aligns (`mx: "auto"`) or resets (`0`), and nothing bleeds past its parent's padding: a table wider than a phone keeps it and scrolls sideways (`TableContainer sx={{ overflowX: "auto" }}`, the sheet's skills and weapons); a heading's gutter is `gutterBottom` (`arkyvree/spacing`). Padding is a box's inner space, never a gap between siblings: inside a surface (a background, a border, a shadow), an indent, a container's space from its own edges, or space that opens and closes with its content (inside a `Collapse`: `AnimatedAlert`'s `gutter`)
- `sx` is written one way (`arkyvree/sx-conventions`): MUI's shorthand keys (`bgcolor`, `p`, `mt`); an object whose values may read the theme (`color: (theme) => …`), never a function of its own; a style on a condition and a caller's `sx` join it as an array (`sx={[{ … }, open && { … }, ...(Array.isArray(sx) ? sx : [sx])]}`); a style wins by its selector, never `!important`, but on a third party's frame (`"& iframe"`), whose inline style no selector beats. The theme's mode is branched on as `theme.palette.mode === "dark"`, in the style it picks; MUI's `useTheme` and `useMediaQuery` are `useIsMobile`'s, and the app's theme mode is `useThemeMode`'s (`contexts/`)

## API Layer

- RPC client (`client/src/services/rpc.ts`) wraps Hono's `hc` client; its fetch throws `ApiError` (`client/src/services/ApiError.ts`) for typed error handling
- `ApiError` includes `status` (HTTP code), `errorName` (server error class name) and `issues` (a save's rules warnings, which `useValidationIssues` shows)
- The API is called through TanStack Query only: a request is made in a function a query or a mutation runs, named `…Fn` as TanStack's `queryFn` and `mutationFn` are, wherever it's handed (`useRulesetSection`'s `createFn`, an editor's `saveFn`, an entity page's `updateFn` and `removeFn`, a `const exportFn` handed to `usePdfExport`, a module's `function deleteEntityFn`; a `…Queries.ts` module's helpers are its queries'; `arkyvree/api-calls-in-queries`, which follows a part of `rpc` a variable holds). A request a click makes is a mutation, and a read it needs is `queryClient.fetchQuery`
- The RPC fetch throws `ApiError` on every non-2xx response, so never check `response.ok`. Read bodies with `parseResponse(rpc.api.x.$get(...))` (from `hono/client`), which also narrows to the success type, and a file's as a blob, `(await rpc.api.x.$get(...)).blob()` (`arkyvree/parsed-responses`). A path is written with dots where a segment is a name (`rpc.api.characters[":id"].share.$post`), brackets only for one that isn't (`[":id"]`, `["class-levels"]`; `arkyvree/dot-notation`). To react to a specific status (e.g. show "not found"), catch `ApiError` and test `error.status`
- Use `InferRequestType` / `InferResponseType` from `hono/client` for all API types — never recreate manually: an endpoint is read off `typeof rpc` (`InferResponseType<(typeof rpc.api.rulesets)[":id"]["$get"], 200>`), and a type already declared is reused, never aliased (`type Option = Ability`)
- A query's options come from a `queryOptions` (or `infiniteQueryOptions`) factory in a `…Queries.ts` module, never written where it runs (`arkyvree/queries`): the caller does `useQuery(rulesetDetailQuery(id))`, or adds its own options (`{ ...featsQuery(rulesetId, filters), placeholderData: keepPreviousData }`). Queries shared across areas live in `client/src/lib/queries.ts` (the lists and the dashboard's statistics, which the sidebar warms; a ruleset, `rulesetDetailQuery`, which a sheet's equipment reads too), so the key, page size and params can't drift apart; a page and the hover that prefetches it in its own area share its area's module (a campaign's card: `campaignDetailQuery`, `pages/campaigns/campaignQueries.ts`), and so does a query a single module reads, beside it (`oglLicenseQueries.ts`, `activityTargetQueries.ts`). A query keeps its previous data with `keepPreviousData`, its durations are named (`lib/durations.ts`: `FIVE_SECONDS`…), and the cache is written in a mutation's callbacks, or by the query's own module (`seedTargetPath`). An infinite query asks for its next page with `getNextPageParam: nextPage`, and its items are `pageItems(data)`, its first page `firstPage(data)` (`lib/pageItems.ts`), read as the component renders; only a value a hook's deps or another query's key read is memoized, on `data` (`useListPageQuery`'s `items`, `useListboxQuery`'s). Ruleset and campaign tab lists do the same in `pages/rulesets/details/sectionQueries.ts` and `pages/campaigns/details/sectionQueries.ts`, what a ruleset's pickers offer in `pages/rulesets/optionQueries.ts` (`aptitudeOptionsQuery`, `featOptionsQuery`, `skillOptionsQuery`, `itemTemplatesQuery`), whatever form or tab offers them, and so do the detail pages that a table row prefetches on hover: the customization page's entity in `pages/rulesets/customization/entityQueries.ts`, the simple entity pages (abilities, skills, saves…) in `pages/rulesets/details/entities/entityDetailQueries.ts`, and the class page and its tabs in `pages/rulesets/details/classes/classSectionQueries.ts`. A page renders with its factory and the hover prefetch (ruleset tab, campaign card, table row) goes through it. Every query key comes from `lib/queryKeys.ts` (`arkyvree/query-keys`: a key written as an array spreads one first), its words in camelCase (`spellsPerDay`)

## State Management

- **Server state**: TanStack React Query for all API data
- **Auth state**: Zustand store (`stores/authStore.ts`): who is signed in, and the emails waiting for a code. Only the user and `isAuthenticated` persist in localStorage (its `partialize`), and `checkSession` asks the server again. The requests that change it are `useAuthRequests`' mutations (sign in, up and out, start a demo, verify, reset; `pending` while any is in flight), and `checkSession` the private routes' probe; the query client leaves their 401s to them. Where the user goes once the session changes is the routes' to say, never a page's: a signed-in user leaves the auth pages for where they were going (`AuthLayoutRoute`: the `redirect` that sent them to sign in, read back by `useAuthRedirect` and kept to this origin by `safeRedirectPath`), a signed-out one leaves the private pages for sign in (`PrivateRoute`, beside it in `components/auth`, its link built by `authPagePath`; `clearSession({ byUser: true })` when the user ended it, who isn't sent back). React Router applies a navigation in a transition, which the store's render runs ahead of: a page navigating as it changes the session loses to the route's redirect
- **Form state**: React Hook Form — use `form.reset()` to populate edit forms, never `key={}` remounting or `defaultValue={}`. A form is made with `useFormWith(defaultValues)` (`hooks/`), never `useForm` (`arkyvree/controlled-inputs`): its values are a whole `T`, every field the form's type requires, which TypeScript checks. A controlled field submits only what the form holds, so a field without a value would reach the server as missing. A ruleset entity's empty form is shared by its create dialog and its editor (`EMPTY_LANGUAGE`… in `pages/rulesets/components/forms/`, `EMPTY_MODIFIER` / `EMPTY_PROPERTY` / `EMPTY_REQUIREMENT` in `components/customization`). Map a nullable text column to `""` in the form values (`description: x.description ?? ""`): an input holds `""`, so `undefined` makes the form dirty on mount. A select's none is `""` too (`SelectField`'s `emptyLabel` stores it), and the API stores either as null; an empty form (`EMPTY_ITEM`, `EMPTY_SPELL`…) holds every field its form shows. A field written in the user's event (what follows from a pick: the slot an item fills) is marked dirty, as its input would be: `setValue(name, value, { shouldDirty: true })`, so the form's Save and its close guard see it. A field's error shows where its binding puts it (`fieldState.error`; the shared fields say it under themselves), never read from `formState.errors` beside it. A rule the API checks on a field is the field's rule (a new feat's or spell's aptitudes: `APTITUDES_RULES`), never a check thrown in `mutationFn`, which would say it in a toast (`arkyvree/controlled-inputs`). Every input is controlled, and a form's field is bound one way: through `useController`, by the shared fields (`NameField`, `DescriptionField`, `EmailField`, `PasswordField`, `FormTextField`, `SelectField`, `SwitchField`; a code's `VerificationCodeField`, a character's `NotesField` and `PrivateNotesField`) or by `Controller` / `useController` around a custom input, never `register` and never a value `watch` reads with a `setValue` for its change (`arkyvree/controlled-inputs`). A custom input passes `field.ref` to the input element (`inputRef`), so a failed submit can focus it. An input component takes its value and its change from its owner and keeps no copy of the value; an editor with state of its own (`ConditionFields`' template mode) seeds it once as it mounts, in a dialog its form was reset for. A field the viewer can't edit is `readOnly` (`slotProps={{ input: { readOnly } }}`, `SelectField`'s `readOnly`): it reads as it is, never a disabled one restyled to look editable (`arkyvree/form-fields`)

## Hooks & Patterns

- `useDebouncedValue(value)` — shared hook for debouncing search inputs (300ms, one delay for every search); a cleared value applies at once, so resetting a search never filters by the old text
- An effect synchronizes with what's outside React (a subscription, a timer, the server through `useFormSync`), and never does what an event or a render does: it never writes a form's field, navigates (a redirect is a rendered `<Navigate>`) nor calls back its owner (an `on…` prop, a callback a ref holds; `arkyvree/effect-writes`). A change happens in the event that causes it (a picked target path seeds its operator in the pick, a picked item fills its slot as its details load), and what follows from data is derived as the component renders (a level's picks fitted to the pools its slots give, `fitPicks.ts`; a race its list no longer offers refused by the form's rules). A dialog's own state is reset by its opener (`form.reset`, the publish dialog's kind) or lives in a body the dialog unmounts as it closes (the extension picker's selection)
- A component's handler and what it derives are plain values, made as it renders (`const handleRowMouseEnter = (row) => …`, a table's columns): `useCallback` and `useMemo` keep only what a hook's deps, a context's value or another query's key read, never what a component is handed, which re-renders with its owner anyway (a section's row handlers, `RulesetSectionTable`'s columns)
- `useOnChange(value, onChange)` (within `hooks/`: `useSearchText` uses it, and the index exports it once a component needs it) — follows a value from outside React's state into the state a component keeps, in the render that sees it change, never an effect that sets state (`react/set-state-in-effect`): `useSearchText`'s text takes the URL's at a navigation. Derive what can be derived during render instead
- `useRulesetSection` — generic CRUD hook for ruleset detail sections (queries, mutations, dialogs, forms); spread its `createDialogProps`, `editDialogProps` and `deleteDialogProps` into the section's `CreateDialog`, `EditDialog` and `EntityDeleteDialog`, overriding `onSubmit` after the spread when the section sends more than its form's values (a requirement's level). Its rows are its `query`'s (a customization tab's, a class's levels), or a paged list's `sectionName`, which its saves refresh; a row's duplicate opens its create dialog on the row's values, `handleDuplicate(row, values)`, which sends them through its `duplicateFn` (a modifier's, an item's), and a create opens on `createDefaults`, which a section may compute (a class's next level, `nextClassLevel`). A save hands what it answered to its `onCreateSuccess` (the created entity, whose page a list opens), `onUpdateSuccess` or `onDeleteSuccess`: a customization tab's follow a copy of an inherited entity through `followCopiesOf(entityId, followCopy)` (`pages/rulesets/followCopies.ts`: its `tag` marks a request with the entity it was sent for, and its `followCopies` are the three callbacks), as the class page's property selects do
- `useRulesetPermissions(ruleset)` (`hooks/`) — the single source of ruleset edit / manage / publish rights on the client: the ruleset's pages, and a character sheet's Create Item, which shows to who may edit its ruleset's items
- `useFormSync(form, values, { key, updatedAt })` — keeps an inline edit form in step with server data without wiping unsaved edits on refetch; use it instead of an effect that calls `form.reset()` whenever the query data changes. Every inline form goes through it, one that edits nothing the server holds (a new password) given its empty values: its `sync.isDirty` enables its `SaveButton`, and while it's true a reload of the page asks first (it calls `useDirtyForm`; no form reads `formState.isDirty` of its own, `arkyvree/dirty-forms`). `key` is the record from the URL (include the ruleset id for ruleset entities: inherited ones keep their id in every fork), so opening another record resets the form. A ruleset entity's page follows a copy-on-write to the copy one way, `useCopyOnWrite(rulesetId, entityId, pathOf)` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`: its `followCopy`, onto the tab shown now, which a class's Levels, Skills and customization tabs follow too, through `useClassCopy`, and its form's `key` and `adoptKey`, the source's key, which a form still holding the source takes over, keeping its unsaved edits; then `forgetSource` once the copy's data is in). Submit through `sync.handleSubmit`, send `sync.updatedAt()` as the save's stale-edit token, and on success call `sync.saved(values, response.updatedAt)` instead of `form.reset()`: a ruleset entity's page does all of it through `useEntitySave`
- `useListParams(sortFields, defaultSort)` — a list page's search and sort from the URL, plus the `searchBarProps` that change them and `updateSearchParams`, which writes the page's other params: the search is the hook's state (`SearchBar` is controlled; the router applies a URL change in a transition, which can't feed a text input), written to the URL as it's typed and taking the URL's at Back or a link, and `search` is it debounced, for the query. `useSearchText(key)` does the same for a section's search. `useSearchParam` for any other filter, and a filter a list's tab offers is in the URL, never in a component's state (a ruleset list's aptitude: `useAptitudeFilter(rulesetId)`, the picked aptitude read back for its picker with its `aptitudeError` (the picker's `loadError`); a spell list's level: `useSpellLevelFilter(allowAll)`, both in `pages/rulesets/hooks`); a param is read through `oneOf()` (`lib/oneOf.ts`), never compared or converted raw (`=== "true"`, `Number(…)`). A picked filter, view or sort pushes a history entry, so Back returns to the one before; typed text replaces its entry (`useSearchText`, `useListParams`), so Back never steps through each letter. Sort options come from `NAME_SORTS` / `CREATED_SORTS` / `UPDATED_SORTS`
- `useNotificationActions` — accept / reject invites, download exports and open targets for any notification surface. Every surface (the page, the dashboard's card, the bell's panel) shows a notification through `NotificationMessage` (`components/notifications`): its message, its details inline under it (a tooltip never opens on a phone), and, where read and unread ones list together, the unread dot (`unread`) with `UNREAD_NOTIFICATION_SX`'s tint on its row. What an activity or a notification changed shows inline under it through `ActivityDetails`, on the activities page too, never in a tooltip (`arkyvree/notification-messages`)
- `useAnswerInvite(onNoLongerPending?)` (`components/invites`) — accepts and rejects an invite of any kind (`inviteKinds.ts`' `INVITE_KINDS`, beside it: its requests, its name in a toast, the list it adds to, its page's facts, and where accepting lands, its `entityPath` or `listPath`, which `acceptedPath(kind, entityId)` picks), one way wherever it's answered: a notification's buttons (`useNotificationActions`, its types `INVITE_NOTIFICATION_TYPES` from `shared/activity.ts`, which Mark All as Read leaves unread on the server) and the invite's own page (`InvitePage`). Its toasts, what it refreshes and an invite no longer pending (a 409: answered or revoked elsewhere) are its own
- `useRulesetAbilities(rulesetId)` (`hooks/`) / `useRulesetSaves(rulesetId, enabled?)` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`) / `useRulesetLanguages(rulesetId, enabled?)` (the sheet's identity section's, `components/characters/sections`) — the ability / save / language lists for pickers and columns (the first 100 of each, the most one request returns; rulesets have far fewer), each one `rulesetOptionsQuery(list, rulesetId)`'s (`lib/queries.ts`); don't query them ad hoc. `useRulesetFeats(rulesetId, search?, enabled?)` (`pages/rulesets/hooks`) is the feat picker's list: a ruleset has hundreds, so it searches the server and pages in as the listbox scrolls
- `useOpenEntity(rulesetId)` — opens a ruleset entity's page with the current list as its Back target; `entityPageState(location.state)` (`pages/rulesets/entityPageState.ts`) reads that target back, and `entityPageBack(location.state, fallback)` gives where Back goes and its name ("Back to Class" from a class's tab, else "Back to Ruleset")
- `useValidationIssues(fallback)` (`components/characters`, the sheet's equipment and the level wizard's) — a save the server can refuse with rules warnings: shows them in the form (with a force save), toasts anything else, `fallback` naming what failed
- `useToggleSet()` — expanded rows / open groups: `keys` (the set), `toggle(key)` one at a time, `clear()`
- `useDialogState<T>(initial?)` — a dialog opened for a record, or for nothing (`true`): `target`, `open`, `openWith`, `close`, `onExited`; opened from the start on `initial` (the sheet's Add Level, as a new character arrives from its create dialog: `characterPageState`'s `openLevelUp`)
- `useAnchorMenu()` — a menu that opens from what was clicked: `anchorEl`, `open`, `openMenu` (the button's click), `closeMenu`, and `closeMenuAnd(then)` for an item's click (`arkyvree/anchor-menus`)
- Infinite listboxes: `useListboxQuery(options)` returns the loaded `items` and the listbox's `onScroll`; give the listbox `ScrollSafeListbox`. Several queries behind one listbox share a `createListboxScrollHandler([...])` (`lib/listboxScroll.ts`); flatten any other infinite query with `pageItems(data)` (`lib/pageItems.ts`)
- A query that can't run yet passes `skipToken` as its `queryFn`, in its factory (not `enabled: !!id`, which `arkyvree/queries` refuses); `enabled` is for plain on/off gates
- Untyped JSON (activity and notification payloads, stored state, a socket's messages: `isWsEvent`, `shared/webSocketEvents.ts`, the server's `WsEvent`) is read through `isRecord` (`shared/isRecord.ts`) guards, not casts, and whether a value is one of a list is `isOneOf(value, list)` (`shared/isOneOf.ts`), which narrows it, never a `.some` comparing each (`arkyvree/one-of`)
- What the browser keeps is a store's (`client/src/stores/`: zustand's `persist`, or a module of its own, `themeModeStorage.ts`), read and written through `browserStorage.ts`'s guards (`readStored`, `writeStored`, `removeStored`), which a blocked storage (a private window, blocked site data) never makes throw: never `localStorage` or `sessionStorage` elsewhere (`arkyvree/browser-storage`). The URL's search params are read through the shared hooks (`useSearchParam`, `useListParams`, `useSearchText`), never `useSearchParams` elsewhere, and a page's router state through its guard (`entityPageState`, `authPageState`, `characterPageState`), never a member of `location.state` (`arkyvree/navigation`). A date is shown through `lib/formatDate.ts` (`formatDate` and `formatDateTime` in the viewer's language; `formatRelativeTime`, "5m ago", in English, past a month its date and time), never formatted in place (`arkyvree/date-formats`)
- Mutations use `.mutate()` with `onSuccess`/`onError` callbacks, not `.mutateAsync()`, and a mutation is held whole (`const saveMutation = useMutation(…)`, `saveMutation.isPending`), never destructured (`arkyvree/client-apis`)

## Toast/Snackbar

- Queue-based `SnackbarProvider` in `contexts/SnackbarProvider.tsx` — notifications process sequentially
- Use `useSnackbar()` hook: `snackbar.success()`, `snackbar.error()`, etc. `info` takes options: an `action` (`{ label, onClick }`, a button on the toast) and `persistent` (it stays until it's dismissed), as the new version's Refresh does (`WebSocketProvider`)
- Never use `console.error` for user-facing errors — always use snackbar. An error's toast names what failed, shown when the error has no message of its own: `snackbar.error(error, "Failed to archive campaign")` and `useValidationIssues("Failed to save item")` require it, and its words start "Failed to". A toast with no error caught isn't an error's: a warning or an info. An error shows through `errorMessage(error, fallback)`, never its raw `message`, and is told apart by its `status` or its `errorName`, never its words (`arkyvree/error-reads`). A server error's name is read once, by a predicate in `lib/errorMessage.ts` beside `accessLost(error)`: `emailNotVerified(error)`, and so is a status a predicate names, a 429 by `rateLimited(error)`
- A toast is a phrase: "Ruleset archived", "This export expired: generate a new one", no final period or "!", no "successfully" nor "Please", never "You have …", and an invite is an "invite", never an "invitation" (`arkyvree/toast-wording`)

## Loading Indicators

- Always use `<DiceSpinner>` (`components/common/DiceSpinner.tsx`) — never MUI `CircularProgress` (`arkyvree/client-apis`)
- For Buttons, use the wrapper API so the button doesn't shrink when loading flips: `<DiceSpinner size="small" loading={isPending}>Save</DiceSpinner>` as the Button child. Pair with `disabled={isPending}`. Keep any static `startIcon` outside the wrapper. Every button that starts a request shows it running this way, an icon button's icon too, and so does an action its owner hands a request to (Proceed Anyway's `pending`; `arkyvree/pending-buttons`). A component whose request is in flight says so by its `pending` (a dialog wrapper's, `DialogFooter`'s, `SaveButton`'s, `AuthSubmitButton`'s, `ActionMenuItem`'s): never `isLoading`, a query's first load (a table's skeleton, `EntityDetailLayout`'s), nor `loading`, which a spinner and MUI's picker say of themselves (`arkyvree/pending-props`).
- A page's first load, a route's `Suspense` fallback too, is a `PageLoader` (a character sheet's page draws the sheet's skeleton instead, `CharacterDetailSkeleton`). A section's or a dialog's loader is a standalone `<DiceSpinner />` (default medium); it centers itself, so give it a section's spacing through `sx` (`<DiceSpinner sx={{ py: 4 }} />`) and nothing else, never a box around it, a height or the large size (`arkyvree/page-loaders`)
- A table's first load is a `TableSkeleton`, its header over rows of skeletons (`RulesetSectionTable`'s, a campaign's players); anything else loads as a section's spinner, a grid's and a tree's too. A skeleton means loading, only: what's empty or hidden (a field its owner hides, a pick not made yet) says so in a `BlankNote` (`arkyvree/skeletons`)

## Dialog Conventions

- Pick the wrapper by purpose — there is no lint rule to choose between them, only a convention:
  - **`CreateDialog` / `EditDialog`** (from `StandardDialogs.tsx`): standard create / edit shapes. Default for create or update flows.
  - **`FormDialog`**: any other dialog that contains a React Hook Form. Binds the form, registers with the global dirty-tracker, blocks backdrop / Escape close while dirty.
  - **`ConfirmDialog` / `DeleteDialog`** (from `StandardDialogs.tsx`): yes/no confirmations, over a page or over the dialog that asks (the level wizard's discard, the share link's revoke). Set its `intent` (`caution` for Archive / Leave, `positive` for Publish, `destructive` for what destroys) and its `confirmLabel`, which it requires, to the action ("Archive Character", never "Confirm"). `DeleteDialog` is the `destructive` preset. A confirmation asks "Are you sure you want to …?", then says what follows; a deletion ends "This action cannot be undone.", or "You can restore it from Local Changes." for what a fork inherits, and an archive "You can unarchive it at any time from the Archived filter.", or "Only its owner can unarchive it." to who archives what they don't own (a ruleset's Admin) (`arkyvree/confirm-wording`): a ruleset's delete is an `EntityDeleteDialog` (`pages/rulesets/components`; an entity page's, its `EntityDetailLayout`'s own), which says which, and its row action and its page's menu say "Delete" or "Delete Permanently" alike (`useRestorableDelete`, `pages/rulesets/hooks`).
  - **`Modal`**: info, manager and picker dialogs that don't bind a form (Link Character to Campaign and Subscribe to Extensions hold their pick in their own state). **Never wrap a `<form>` in `Modal`** — Modal skips the dirty-close guard, so backdrop click / Escape discards typed input (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`). The level wizard (`LevelWizardDialog`) is the one `Modal` around a form, whose steps hold no `<form>`: it guards its own close, ignoring a backdrop click and asking before Cancel or Escape discards its progress (a `ConfirmDialog`): Add Level's once a class is planned, Edit Level's once a pick changed, as an edit form's dirty state says.
- A dialog is `sm`, its wrappers' default: a plain form (a name, a description, a select: Create New Class, Create New Mechanic, Create Variants), a picker (Subscribe to Extensions), a confirmation, a text (License & Attribution), a manager (both contributors'). It's `md` when its form has many fields in two columns (the spell's, a new character's) or it shows a target path or a requirement tree (a modifier's, a requirement's, an inventory item's, a character's Manage Modifiers, the level wizard), and never `xs`, which `arkyvree/dialog-conventions` refuses
- A dialog's lead line, the first thing it says, is a `DialogContentText` (body1, grey), as a confirmation's. Its content keeps the theme's padding, its title right above it (in its `<form>` when it has one), and its first block sits 8px under the title (`sx={{ pt: 1 }}` on the column it opens with), as a create dialog's fields (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`)
- Dialog title and action-bar styling come from the theme — use a plain `<DialogTitle>` of its words, no icon (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`), without extra typography or padding, and end the dialog with a `DialogFooter` (`onCancel`, its `cancelLabel` "Close" when there's nothing to confirm, an `action` with its `label`, `intent`, `icon`, `disabled` and `onClick` (a submit without), `pending` while it saves, and its own steps as children, before the action: the level wizard's Back, its Next and Finish being its `action`), never `DialogActions` by hand, and its action is contained, as every dialog's is (`arkyvree/dialog-footers`).
- Manager dialogs with both a list and an invite form: keep the outer `Modal` for the manager and put the form in a nested `FormDialog` opened from an Invite button.
- Edit dialogs receive a `form` prop (from React Hook Form) — parent calls `form.reset({ ...data })` before opening. A create dialog's form is reset as it opens too (`useRulesetSection`'s `handleCreate`), never after a save, so a cancelled one reopens empty
- A create dialog's title is "Create New …" ("Create New Language", "Create New Character"), an edit dialog's "Edit …"; a dialog that does something else is titled with its action ("Fork Ruleset"). Four create dialogs say it otherwise today: "Add Player", a character's "Add Modifier", "Add Item to Inventory" and "Create Requirement"; one titled "Add …" submits in its words ("Add Player", "Add Modifier", "Add Item") and toasts "… added", never the default "Create" (`arkyvree/dialog-conventions`)
- A dialog stays mounted, its `open` showing it, so it fades out as it closes. A dialog about a record (a row, a level) opens through `useDialogState` (`dialog.openWith(row)`, `open={dialog.open}`, `dialog.target`), which keeps the record while it fades, never an open flag beside a record of its own; one that keeps state of its own (the level wizards) mounts with that record (`{dialog.target && …}`) and lets it go once faded (`onExited`), so it opens clean (`arkyvree/dialog-mounts`)
- A dialog that sends a request closes in its mutation's `onSuccess`; when the request fails it stays open, its toast saying why, so the user can retry or cancel (a confirmation too: never closed in `onError` or `onSettled`, nor as it sends: `mutate(x, { onSuccess })`, `arkyvree/confirm-dialogs`)
- A form dialog doesn't receive the record it edits as a prop — its form state is the source of truth; a confirmation receives what it names (`RemoveContributorDialog`'s `contributor`)
- A dialog that holds a form never loses it to a stray click: `FormDialog` (which `CreateDialog` and `EditDialog` are built on) ignores a backdrop click or Escape while its form's values differ from its defaults (`valuesEqual`, so a value set without `shouldDirty` counts), and any close while it's `pending`; it registers an open, changed form with `useDirtyForm`, so a reload asks first. A form dialog is one of them, never a guard written again in `onClose`; one that prefills on open does it with `form.reset(values)`, which makes them its defaults

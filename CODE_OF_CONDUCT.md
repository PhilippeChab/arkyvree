# Code of Conduct

Arkyvree welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participation
in this project should be respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Make room for different experiences, abilities, and perspectives.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect people's privacy, boundaries, and time. Contributions and support are voluntary.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, targeted
humiliation, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable. Neither are repeated unwanted contact, deliberate disruption, or
retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Where this applies

These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.
They apply to maintainers as well as contributors.

## Reporting a concern

Email **[philippe.chab@outlook.com](mailto:philippe.chab@outlook.com)** with the
subject “Arkyvree conduct report”. Include relevant links, what happened, and
any immediate safety concerns. Share only information you are comfortable
providing. Do not post sensitive incident details in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as privately as practicable. Information may need to be
shared with people necessary to investigate or respond; absolute confidentiality
cannot be promised. Good-faith reporting must not result in retaliation.

For abuse involving GitHub itself, including concerns you cannot safely raise
with the maintainer, you can also use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Moderation

The maintainer may remove harmful content, request a correction, issue a warning,
restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude someone. The response
will take severity, context, and repeated behavior into account. Serious safety
violations may warrant immediate exclusion rather than a sequence of warnings.

If you believe a moderation decision was mistaken, send a private explanation
to the reporting address. Do not contact other participants against their wishes.
